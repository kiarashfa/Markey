Shown at the Tokyo Motor Show in October 2009, and the point at which the car
that became the [Toyota 86](/Markey/cars/toyota-86-zn6/) stopped being an idea
about a hybrid V6 and became a light rear-drive coupé with a Subaru
boxer.[@wikipedia-toyota-86]

Its predecessor concept, the FT-HS of 2007, was bigger and hybrid-powered.
Between the two, Toyota bought 16.5% of Fuji Heavy Industries; the FT-86 that
resulted used a Subaru chassis derived from the Impreza and the new D-4S boxer
engine, with the body refined by Toyota's ED² studio in Nice. The production
car arrived at the same show two years later.

No performance figures are recorded here because Toyota published none — not
even the manufacturer claims a concept usually carries.
