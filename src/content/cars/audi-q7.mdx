Audi's first SUV, launched in 2005 on a platform shared with the Volkswagen
Touareg and Porsche Cayenne, and the car that introduced the world's only
series-production V12 diesel passenger car.[@wikipedia-audi-q7] It has run
through two full generations plus a third announced for 2026, growing a
plug-in hybrid line in its second generation.

This entry covers the launch 4L generation and the long-running second
generation (4M); the incoming third generation is not authored here.
