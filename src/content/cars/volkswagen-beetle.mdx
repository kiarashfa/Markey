Ferdinand Porsche's brief, set by the Nazi government in 1934, was a car
that could carry two adults and three children at speed while using no more
than 7 litres of fuel per 100 km. That was Germany's own answer to a car every family
could own.[@wikipedia-volkswagen-beetle] It stayed in production, essentially
unchanged in concept, for sixty-five years and 21.5 million cars, the longest
production run and the highest total of any car built on one platform.
