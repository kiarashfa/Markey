Launched 7 May 1976 as a three-door hatchback, the first Accord was a
platform expansion of the Civic aimed at buyers who wanted more room and
comfort without leaving Honda's small-car efficiency behind.[@wikipedia-honda-accord]
A four-door sedan followed in October 1977, using the same wheelbase and
underpinnings; by 1982 the car had made history as the first Japanese-brand
car built in the United States, at Honda's new Marysville, Ohio plant.

The same 1.6-litre engine was rated differently depending on market and
emissions equipment — 80 PS under Japan's JIS standard and Europe's stricter
DIN standard, but only 68 hp under the US's SAE net standard for
emissions-equipped American cars — which is recorded here as a market
override on the same trim rather than three unrelated figures.
