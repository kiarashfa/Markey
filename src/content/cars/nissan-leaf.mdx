The world's first mass-produced, series-built battery-electric car, on sale
continuously since 2010, years before most rivals treated an EV as
anything but a low-volume compliance exercise.[@wikipedia-nissan-leaf] Its
name is a backronym: "leading environmentally-friendly affordable family
car". This entry covers the original ZE0 and the substantially redesigned
ZE1, which added real range and a larger e+ battery option; the third
generation, launched in 2025 as a crossover rather than a hatchback, is not
covered here.
