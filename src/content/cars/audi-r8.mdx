Audi's only mid-engined production supercar, built from 2006 to 2024 across
two generations that shared their underpinnings with a Lamborghini in both
cases: the Gallardo, then the Huracán.[@wikipedia-audi-r8] It shares its name,
confusingly, with the unrelated Audi R8 Le Mans Prototype racing car that won
Le Mans repeatedly in the early 2000s; the road car borrowed the name rather
than any of the engineering. Both generations catalogued here were sold with
naturally aspirated V10 engines to the very end (the R8 never went
turbocharged, even as most of the rest of Audi's range did), and Audi ended
the nameplate in 2024 without a direct successor.
