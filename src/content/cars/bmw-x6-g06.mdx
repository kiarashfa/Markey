The current X6, sharing BMW's CLAR platform with the X5 and X7, unveiled in
2019 with a larger, illuminated kidney grille and, for the first time on any
BMW, an optional Vantablack show finish that never reached
production.[@wikipedia-bmw-x6] The M50i's twin-turbo V8 was renamed M60i for
2023 when BMW switched from the N63 engine to the mild-hybrid S68, keeping
the same power figures across the change.

The source's engine tables for this generation give 0-100 km/h times
directly in the metric unit this site stores, alongside power already
expressed in kW. This is a rare case of a manufacturer-facing English Wikipedia
table needing no unit conversion at all.
