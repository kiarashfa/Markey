Honda's move upmarket from the Civic, launched in 1976 and sold continuously
ever since — its second-longest-running nameplate after the Civic
itself.[@wikipedia-honda-accord] It grew from a compact hatchback into a
mid-size saloon that spent sixteen years, 1982 to 1997, as the best-selling
Japanese car in the United States, and became the first car built under an
import badge to top America's sales charts outright, in 1989. Eleven
generations later it is sold as a saloon only, no longer as a hatchback,
coupe or wagon.
