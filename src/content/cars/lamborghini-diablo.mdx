The Diablo replaced the Countach in 1990 and was Lamborghini's flagship for
eleven years, styled first by Marcello Gandini and then reworked at Chrysler's
studio after Chrysler bought the company mid-development.[@wikipedia-lamborghini-diablo]
It was the first production Lamborghini to top 200 mph, and the range grew
through a rear-drive/all-wheel-drive split (VT) and a 1999 facelift before
giving way to the Murciélago in 2001.

VT 6.0 that make up the whole run.
