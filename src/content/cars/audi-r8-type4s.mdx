The second and final R8, launched in 2015 on the Modular Sports System
platform it shared with the Lamborghini Huracán, and sold until Audi retired
the nameplate altogether in 2024.[@wikipedia-audi-r8-type-4s] The V8 option
was dropped entirely (every Type 4S is a V10), and no manual gearbox was
offered, both firsts for the car.

The trim recorded here, the V10 RWD introduced in 2019, is unusual for an R8:
Audi's quattro system had been standard or optional on every version of the
car until a limited-run rear-drive RWS model in 2017 proved popular enough
that a permanent rear-drive V10 followed. Dropping the front differential and
driveshafts saves 65 kg over the equivalent all-wheel-drive coupé and shifts
the weight balance to a 40:60 front/rear split, the only weight-distribution
figure Wikipedia's article states directly for this generation.
