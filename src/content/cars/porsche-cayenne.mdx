Porsche's first SUV, on sale since 2002 and the car that funded much of the
sports-car range that followed it.[@wikipedia-porsche-cayenne] It was
Porsche's first V8-engined model since the 928 ended production in 1995,
its first vehicle with four doors, and its first off-road-capable model
since the Junior and Super tractors of the 1950s — developed jointly with
Volkswagen, sharing its platform with the Touareg and, later, the Audi Q7.

This entry covers the first two generations: the 9PA of 2002, developed
alongside the Touareg from the ground up, and the 92A of 2010, lighter and
lower than its predecessor and the first to offer a plug-in hybrid variant.
The third generation (9YA, 2017-present) is a gap in this catalogue.
