The fourth generation, and the first sold in Japan under the Yaris name
itself — the Vitz badge it inherited was retired at launch.[@wikipedia-toyota-yaris-xp210]
Built on Toyota's dedicated small-car TNGA-B platform, it also underpins the
GR Yaris rally homologation car and, since 2022, a rebadged Mazda2 Hybrid —
an unusual direction for a platform-sharing arrangement that normally runs
the other way.

Dimensions here are the Europe-market figures, which differ from the
Japan-market car by tens of millimetres in every direction — Toyota held the
JDM car to Japan's "compact car" width class deliberately. The hybrid's
85 kW is a published combined system figure; the engine alone makes only
67-68 kW, and the two cannot be added because they peak at different points.
