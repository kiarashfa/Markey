Announced in February 2021 on the CMF-CD platform shared with the Nissan
X-Trail and Renault Austral, the third-generation Qashqai grew modestly in
every dimension and introduced Nissan's e-Power hybrid system to the
range.[@wikipedia-nissan-qashqai] e-Power is a series hybrid: a 1.5-litre
variable-compression petrol engine never drives the wheels directly, existing
solely to generate electricity for a separate traction motor.

That architecture is why no e-Power trim is recorded here. The source states
the generator engine's output (154 hp) and the electric motor's output (187
hp) separately, and never publishes a combined system figure, and because
the two units serve entirely different jobs, adding them together would
invent a number nobody has published, exactly the trap this site's policy on
hybrid outputs exists to avoid.
