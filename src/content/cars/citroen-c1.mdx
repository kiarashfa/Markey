Citroën's city car from 2005 to 2022, one of three near-identical
triplets (alongside the Peugeot 107/108 and Toyota Aygo) built on a shared
platform at a joint-venture factory in the Czech Republic.[@wikipedia-citroen-c1]
It ran for two generations, both built at the same plant, before Citroën
discontinued it without a direct replacement as the market for city cars
this small contracted. This catalogue covers both.
