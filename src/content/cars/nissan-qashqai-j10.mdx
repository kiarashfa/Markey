Unveiled as a concept at the 2004 Geneva Motor Show and put into production
for February 2007, the first Qashqai sold more than 1.24 million units in
Europe over a seven-year run built entirely at Nissan's Sunderland
plant.[@wikipedia-nissan-qashqai] A longer, seven-seat Qashqai+2 arrived in
2008, extending the wheelbase by 135 mm and the body by 211 mm rather than
just adding a third row to the existing shell.

The mid-life switch from a 2.0-litre to a 1.6-litre diesel in 2010 is recorded
here as two separate trims: the smaller engine made less peak power (96 kW
against 110 kW) for the same 320 N-m of torque, delivered lower in the rev
range, and Nissan said at the time it was quicker in-gear despite the lower
headline figure. This is a case where the smaller number does not mean the slower
car.
