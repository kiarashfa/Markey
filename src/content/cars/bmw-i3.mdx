BMW's first purpose-built electric car, sold from 2013 to 2022 as the debut
model of the standalone "i" sub-brand.[@wikipedia-bmw-i3-hatchback] Rather
than converting an existing 3 Series or 1 Series, BMW built the i3 around a
dedicated aluminium chassis carrying a carbon-fibre-reinforced-plastic
passenger cell — a construction method never before used at this production
volume — with rear-hinged "coach doors" and no B-pillar.

Only one generation was built, badged internally I01, so this catalogue's
hub-and-generation structure holds a single entry here: one nameplate, sold
throughout its life as a pure battery-electric car and, optionally, with a
small petrol range-extender engine that charged the battery but never drove
the wheels directly.
