A Brazil-only car that reused a retired name rather than continuing a
lineage: engineered in Brazil and co-designed with Fiat's Turin studio, built
on the Fiat Palio-derived Economy platform, and never exported to
Europe.[@wikipedia-fiat-uno] Both of its flex-fuel engines run on any mix of
petrol and ethanol, standard practice for the Brazilian market and one this
site's fuel-economy fields are not built to describe, which is why none are
recorded here.

The source states this generation's two engine outputs only as "74 hp" and
"87 hp," without saying whether that means metric PS or mechanical
horsepower. Both readings are within 1.4% of each other, but rather than
silently pick one, this entry converts on the metric-PS reading (standard
for Brazilian flex-fuel spec sheets) and states the alternative and its size
in each figure's own note.
