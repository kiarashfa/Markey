The Touareg was Volkswagen's first proper off-road SUV, launched in 2002 on
a platform shared with the original Porsche Cayenne and Audi Q7 and sold
with engines ranging from a four-cylinder diesel up to a naturally
aspirated V10 turbodiesel and a 6.0-litre W12.[@wikipedia-volkswagen-touareg]
Volkswagen never sold the third generation in North America, where the
larger, cheaper Atlas covered the same ground instead, and the whole
Touareg line ended production in March 2026 after three generations and
almost 24 years. This catalogue covers all three.
