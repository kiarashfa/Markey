The seventh and final Celica dropped the notchback coupé body entirely,
selling only as a liftback, and — for the first time in the nameplate's
history — offered no all-wheel-drive GT-Four variant.[@wikipedia-toyota-celica]
It was part of Toyota's Project Genesis, an unsuccessful attempt to attract
younger buyers to Toyota dealerships in the United States.

The two trims recorded here are the Japanese-market SS-I and SS-II
specifications, since the article's most complete power-and-torque table is
for that market; UK and other export versions of the same engines carried
slightly different outputs (142 PS rather than 145 PS for the base 1ZZ-FE,
for instance), which are not recorded as separate trims here.
