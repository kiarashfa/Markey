The third-generation C4, revealed in June 2020 with "coupe SUV" styling on
an all-new EMP1 platform shared with the Peugeot 2008 and 208.[@wikipedia-citroen-c4]
A fastback-saloon twin, the C4 X, followed in 2022, sharing the same
wheelbase and most of the same mechanicals under a different rear end.

Three combustion trims and the battery-electric e-C4 are recorded here.
Kerb weight is a genuine gap for this generation: unlike the previous two,
whose Wikipedia infoboxes both publish a weight figure, this one gives none
at all, in the body text or the specification table, so the field is left
as an explicit placeholder.
