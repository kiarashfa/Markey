The original Civic, built to replace the tiny N600 and roughly double its
displacement while keeping the same basic idea: light, front-wheel drive,
transversely mounted.[@wikipedia-honda-civic-first-generation] Its CVCC
engine variant, launched in 1975, could meet strict new American emissions
rules without a catalytic converter — which meant it could run on leaded
fuel when many rivals could not, a real advantage during the era's gasoline
shortages.

The launch engine's power figure is the one soft spot in this entry:
Wikipedia's own article gives it only as "roughly 50 hp", not a certified
number, so it is recorded here as `estimated` rather than presented with
false precision.
