The original Mondeo, on sale from November 1992, replaced three different
cars in three different regions at once — the Sierra in Europe, the Tempo
and Mercury Topaz in North America, and the Telstar across Asia, Australia
and South Africa — from a single shared CDW27 platform.[@wikipedia-ford-mondeo-first-generation]
In North America it was sold under entirely different names, as the Ford
Contour and Mercury Mystique.

Three trims are recorded here: the 1.8 Zetec petrol, the 1.8 Endura-D
diesel, and the ST200 of 1999 — a Special Vehicle Team-tuned 2.5-litre V6
that replaced the ST24 as the range's performance flagship, badged Contour
SVT in North America. The ST200's own 0-60 mph time is not converted to a
0-100 km/h figure here, since the two are different speed thresholds; only
its manufacturer-quoted top speed, a straightforward mph-to-km/h
conversion, is recorded.
