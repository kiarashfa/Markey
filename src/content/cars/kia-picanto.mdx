Kia's city car since 2003, built on a shortened Hyundai Getz platform and
sold under a shifting set of names depending on market: Morning in South
Korea and much of Asia, Naza Suria and Naza Picanto for a Malaysian
rebadge.[@wikipedia-kia-picanto] It has always competed directly with its
Hyundai stablemate, the i10, sharing engines and, at times, whole platforms
with it. This catalogue covers the original SA and the current
third-generation JA, on sale since 2017.
