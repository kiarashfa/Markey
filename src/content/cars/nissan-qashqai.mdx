The car that talked Europe into crossovers: a "mini-Murano" built to give
C-segment hatchback buyers the look of an SUV without the size or running
costs of Nissan's own X-Trail, on sale since 2006 and never out of
production since.[@wikipedia-nissan-qashqai] Sold as the Dualis in Japan and
Australia for its first generation and briefly as the Rogue Sport in the
United States, it settled on the Qashqai name everywhere by its third
generation, which also introduced Nissan's e-Power series-hybrid system to
the range for the first time.
