One body, one basic layout, three engines across almost three decades:
a 594 cc flat-twin at launch in October 1972, enlarged to 652 cc in 1977 at
an unchanged claimed output, and finally a water-cooled 704 cc unit from 1987
in the hatchback-bodied 126 BIS, which also brought the car's only real
structural change — a rear hatch, replacing the boot lid, over an engine that
stayed exactly where it had always been.[@wikipedia-fiat-126] Fiat built it in
Italy until 1980; FSM in Poland kept building it, under licence and then as
Fiat Auto Poland, for twenty more years after that.

Most of what is verifiable and precisely dated in the source concerns the
Polish-market 126p rather than the original Italian car, which is reflected
here in the market-variant naming on each trim rather than in separate
entries — the underlying mechanical specification is the same car under both
names.
