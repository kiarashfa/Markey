A small hatchback built around one idea Honda kept across four generations
and 25 years: move the fuel tank from under the rear seats to under the
front ones, and the whole load floor and rear-seat geometry gets more
flexible for it.[@wikipedia-honda-fit] Sold as the Fit in Japan and the
Americas and the Jazz almost everywhere else, it has been withdrawn from
North America since the third generation but remains on sale in Japan,
Europe and China, now offered mainly as a hybrid.
