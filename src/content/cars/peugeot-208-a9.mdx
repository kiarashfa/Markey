Launched in 2012 on the older PF1 platform under a team led by Pierre
Authier, the first 208 arrived first as a three-door built at Peugeot's new
Trnava plant in Slovakia, with the five-door following once French
production started a few months later.[@wikipedia-peugeot-208] It carried
over some four-cylinder engines from the outgoing 207 alongside new
three-cylinder units, and its GTi flagship (built around a 1.6-litre
turbo shared in detuned form with the Mini Cooper S) was sold in a lower-
output standard form and a limited 30th Anniversary edition.

Three trims are recorded here: the mainstream 1.2 VTi, the GTi in its
standard 200 PS tune, and the 1.6 e-HDi diesel. Every power figure in the
source's engine tables is explicitly labelled PS, so the conversions here
carry no reading ambiguity, unlike several of Peugeot's earlier B- and
C-segment cars.
