Mazda's answer to the question of what a rotary engine was actually for: a
light, front-mid-engined, rear-drive coupé built around a Wankel engine small
enough and smooth-revving enough to justify the fuel and oil consumption that
sank the rotary everywhere else it was tried.[@wikipedia-mazda-rx-7] Three
generations ran from 1978 to 2002, each one heavier and more highly strung
than the last, before Mazda replaced it with the four-seat RX-8 rather than a
fourth RX-7.
