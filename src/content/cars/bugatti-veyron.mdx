The car that reopened the modern Bugatti marque: a quad-turbocharged W16
engine driving all four wheels, built to combine an unheard-of top speed
with everyday, one-pedal, air-conditioned usability rather than the
stripped-out feel of a typical hypercar.[@wikipedia-bugatti-veyron] Only
around 450 were built across a single generation between 2005 and 2015,
before the Veyron was replaced by the Chiron. This entry treats it, like
other nameplates that never received a numbered second generation, as one
generation entry spanning the whole run.
