The Ibiza has been SEAT's best-selling model since 1984, when it launched as
the first car the newly independent Spanish manufacturer developed on its
own, albeit with Italian styling from Italdesign, German industrialisation
from Karmann, and a gearbox and powertrain developed with Porsche.[@wikipedia-seat-ibiza]
Five generations have followed, spanning SEAT's transition from a
state-owned licensee of Fiat designs to a full Volkswagen Group marque. This
catalogue covers the original Mk1, the "arrow design" Mk4 that carried
SEAT's FR and Cupra hot-hatch badges, and the current Mk5.
