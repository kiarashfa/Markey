The sixth and current generation, launched in India in March 2023 as the
Verna and progressively rolled out elsewhere as the Accent, continuing the split
naming the model has carried for two decades.[@wikipedia-hyundai-accent] It
is built in India and Vietnam rather than South Korea, reflecting where
Hyundai now expects most of its sales to come from.

Two petrol engines are offered: a naturally aspirated 1.5-litre and a
1.5-litre turbo good for a claimed 8.1-second 0-100 km/h with the dual-clutch
gearbox. Kerb weight is a genuine gap here rather than an oversight: neither
Hyundai's own specification pages nor the Wikipedia article publish a figure
for this generation, so the field is left as an explicit placeholder rather
than estimated from the outgoing car.
