The original i30, announced by the Hyundai Arnejs concept at the 2006 Paris
Motor Show and launched for real in mid-2007.[@wikipedia-hyundai-i30] It was
designed in Rüsselsheim, Germany rather than Korea, and sold as an estate
too, the i30cw, which crossed the Atlantic as the Elantra Touring, a name
that shared nothing with the regular i30 hatchback badge used everywhere
else.

Kerb weight is a genuine gap for this generation: unlike the second and
third generations, whose Wikipedia infoboxes both give a weight range, the
first generation's does not give one at all, so the field is left as an
explicit placeholder rather than estimated from a later car.
