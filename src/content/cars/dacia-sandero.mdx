The hatchback companion to the Logan saloon, developed on a slightly
shorter version of the same B0 platform and revealed first in Brazil, as a
Renault, in December 2007. It was the first Renault-badged model ever launched
outside Europe before its own European Dacia debut a few months
later.[@wikipedia-dacia-sandero] It has consistently offered a genuine
factory bi-fuel petrol/LPG engine option across generations, unusual for a
mainstream European hatchback. This catalogue covers the original B90 and
the current third-generation BJI.
