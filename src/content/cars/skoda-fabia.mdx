Škoda's supermini, on sale since 1999 as the replacement for the Felicia
and the first Škoda developed jointly with Volkswagen from a clean
sheet.[@wikipedia-skoda-fabia] It won What Car?'s UK Car of the Year in
2000 and, in its third generation, the outright 2015 What Car? Car of the
Year award as well.

This entry covers the first generation (6Y, 1999), home to the Fabia RS
(the first hot hatch sold exclusively with a diesel engine and no petrol
equivalent), and the third generation (NJ, 2014), which dropped the RS
name entirely after weak sales of its predecessor. The second generation
(5J, 2007) is a gap in this catalogue.
