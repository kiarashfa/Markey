"Recreational Active Vehicle with 4-wheel-drive" — a car-based, unibody
alternative to the body-on-frame SUVs of 1994, credited with defining the
compact crossover segment it still leads three decades
later.[@wikipedia-toyota-rav4] It has grown from a light, short-wheelbase
three-door available with a soft top to a five-seat family crossover with
hybrid and plug-in hybrid drivetrains. This entry covers the original XA10
and the fifth-generation XA50, which introduced the plug-in hybrid RAV4
Prime.
