The 207 was Peugeot's supermini for a single generation, 2006 to 2015,
bridging the 206 and the 208 without a numbering gap and sharing its
platform with the Citroën C3.[@wikipedia-peugeot-207] It was sold as a
three- or five-door hatchback, an estate (the 207 SW) and a folding-hardtop
convertible (the 207 CC), and it carried Peugeot's GTi hot-hatch badge in a
turbocharged engine developed jointly with BMW — the same unit used in the
contemporary Mini Cooper S. This catalogue records the one generation the
207 ever had, with its main engine and trim changes captured as trims rather
than as separate entries.
