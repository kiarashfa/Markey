The fifth car sold as an A6, on sale from 2018 to 2025, and the first
generation where every engine — four-cylinder to the RS6's twin-turbo V8 —
comes with at least 48-volt mild-hybrid assistance as standard, plug-in
hybrid TFSI e variants further up the range.[@wikipedia-audi-a6] It followed
the C7 onto Audi's MLB Evo platform, shared with the A7, A8 and Q7 family.

The trim recorded here, the 45 TFSI quattro, is a mainstream turbocharged
four-cylinder rather than the S6/RS6 performance flagships or the plug-in
hybrid TFSI e variants also sold on this platform. Wikipedia's C8 section
gives no kerb weight, unlike the C4's infobox, so that figure is left out
here rather than estimated from a different market or trim.
