Porsche's first four-door saloon since the stillborn 989 concept of the
1980s, on sale since April 2009 as a five-door liftback rather than a
conventional three-box sedan.[@wikipedia-porsche-panamera] It sits above
the Cayenne in Porsche's range and shares little mechanically with it,
built instead on dedicated Volkswagen Group platforms of its own.

This entry covers the first two generations: the 970 of 2009, whose
liftback proportions drew criticism that Porsche visibly addressed with the
next car, and the 971 of 2016, restyled with a clearer link to the 911's
tail-light treatment. The current third generation (972, 2024-present) is a
gap in this catalogue.
