Audi's Bauhaus-influenced sports coupé ran for twenty-five years and three
generations, from 1998 to 2023, before Audi discontinued it with no direct
successor.[@wikipedia-audi-tt] Its rounded, minimal first-generation shape
made it an instant design landmark; a series of high-speed accidents also
forced a mid-life recall to add a rear spoiler and stability control.

This entry covers the launch 8N generation and the final 8S generation; the
8J generation (2006) in between is not authored here.
