The original Tucson, revealed in 2004 as a unibody crossover positioned
below the Santa Fe, sharing its Elantra-derived platform with the
second-generation Kia Sportage.[@wikipedia-hyundai-tucson] It was built and
sold far beyond Korea: assembled in China, India and Brazil, and marketed
in Japan simply as the "Hyundai JM."

Three engines are recorded here: a 2.0-litre four, a 2.7-litre V6, and a
2.0-litre CRDi diesel, each in its original tune of 2004-2006. A more
powerful, 140/150 PS tune of the same diesel engine arrived from 2006, and
is a different figure describing a later car; it is not the number
attached to this trim. The car's kerb weight is only published as a range
across the whole line-up (1,470-1,529 kg), so the lowest figure is recorded
at generation level rather than assigned to any one trim.
