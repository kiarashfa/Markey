The current Civic, sold since 2021 as a sedan across all markets and as a
liftback hatchback in North America, Europe and Turkey, built on a widened
and stiffened version of the previous generation's
platform.[@wikipedia-honda-civic-eleventh-generation] North America launched
with a choice of a naturally aspirated 2.0-litre base engine or a
turbocharged 1.5-litre in higher trims; the 2.0-litre was retired for the
2025 model year and replaced by a hybrid powertrain, which this entry does
not yet cover as a trim.

Both recorded trims are North America-market figures, published in
horsepower and pound-feet and converted here to kW and N⋅m; the article does
not give separately certified outputs for other markets, so no market
variant is recorded. Dimensions and kerb weight are likewise given as
per-trim or per-body-style ranges in the source; this entry stores the
lowest figure in each range, per the project's convention, and notes the
full spread in the data file rather than in the prose.
