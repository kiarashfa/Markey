Peugeot's C-segment family hatchback, on sale since 2007 as the successor
to the 307 and, across three generations, sold as a hatchback, an estate, a
coupé cabriolet and — in China — a saloon.[@wikipedia-peugeot-308] A 308
HDi once held the Guinness World Record for the most fuel-efficient
mainstream car after averaging 3.13 L/100 km over a 14,580 km run, though
Peugeot was later rapped by UK advertising regulators for quoting that
figure in ordinary advertising.

This entry covers the first two generations: the T7 of 2007, whose GTi/GT
revived the performance-hatch name after a hiatus, and the T9 of 2013,
whose GTi by Peugeot Sport was sold in two distinct power outputs. The
current third generation (P5, 2021-present), sold alongside plug-in hybrid
and full-electric versions, is a gap in this catalogue.
