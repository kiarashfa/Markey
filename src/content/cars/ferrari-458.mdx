The 458 replaced the F430 in 2009 and was the last naturally aspirated V8
Ferrari before the 488 switched to turbocharging.[@wikipedia-ferrari-458] It
brought direct fuel injection to a mid-engined Ferrari road car for the first
time and, in the 458 Speciale, a track-focused variant with active
aerodynamics developed with input from former Ferrari F1 driver Michael
Schumacher.

A single generation, recorded here as the launch 458 Italia alongside the
458 Speciale, its higher-output, lighter derivative.
