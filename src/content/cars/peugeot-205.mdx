The supermini that turned Peugeot's fortunes around, on sale from February
1983 to 1999 without ever changing generation.[@wikipedia-peugeot-205] It
replaced the ageing 104 and the Talbot Samba, won What Car?'s Car of the
Year for 1984, and was later named CAR Magazine's "car of the decade" — and
its GTI derivative is still routinely cited as the car that defined the hot
hatch as a category.

This nameplate had only one generation, so the hub-and-generation structure
here holds a single entry spanning the whole 1983-1999 run, with the car's
considerable range of trims — from a 954 cc entry hatchback to the Group B
homologation-special Turbo 16 — recorded as trims within it.
