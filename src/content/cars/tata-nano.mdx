Announced in 2008 as the cheapest new car sold anywhere (one lakh rupees, or
about $2,500) and built for the millions of Indian families who could afford
a motorcycle but not a car.[@wikipedia-tata-nano] It stayed in production for
a decade before Tata ended it in 2018, without a direct successor.
