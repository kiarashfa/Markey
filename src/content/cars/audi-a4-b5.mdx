The car that renamed the Audi 80: launched in October 1994 on a new
Volkswagen Group B5 platform shared with the contemporary Passat, and widely
credited within Audi and Volkswagen for the build quality that made the A4
the company-wide benchmark other models were measured against.[@wikipedia-audi-a4]
It also introduced the 1.8-litre, five-valve-per-cylinder turbo engine
recorded on the trim here: the first Volkswagen Group engine with that head
design, developed by Audi Sport.

The B5's generation infobox on Wikipedia leaves kerb weight blank; the figure
used here instead comes from NHTSA's Canadian Vehicle Specifications for the
1996 model year, which is why it is attributed to a different source than the
rest of the entry.
