Peugeot's 206 sold in huge numbers for a decade, but its 2006 successor
never quite matched its predecessor's reputation: Autocar called the 207
"safely played" and lacking in charm, and it was rated one of the least
reliable cars in its class by one insurer.[@wikipedia-peugeot-207] It sold
well regardless, launching in three markets simultaneously and reaching
sixth place in overall UK sales for 2007. The 207 CC folding-hardtop
convertible fared better with reviewers, and the range's Super 2000 rally
variant continued Peugeot's competition programme after the 206 World Rally
Car.

Four trims are recorded: the everyday 1.6 VTi petrol, the turbocharged GTi
in its two power outputs either side of the 2009 facelift, and the 1.6 HDi
diesel. The GTi's outputs are explicitly labelled bhp (mechanical
horsepower) in the source; the diesel's is not labelled at all, so it is
recorded as an estimate read against the PS convention the HDi range was
usually quoted in. No exact displacement in cubic centimetres is published
for any engine here, only the nominal "1.6 litre" label, so none is invented
— it is folded into each engine's description instead of stored as a
figure.
