The original i10, unveiled in New Delhi in October 2007 as a replacement for
the ageing Atos and developed primarily with the Indian market in
mind.[@wikipedia-hyundai-i10] It shared its length and wheelbase almost
exactly with the Atos it replaced, but was wider and lower, and it went on to
be built and sold well beyond India: in Malaysia as an Inokom, in Mexico as
a Dodge, and across Europe under its own name.

Only one trim's figures are recorded with confidence here: the 1.1-litre
petrol engine's 48 kW is stated directly in the source. A 1.2-litre Kappa
engine and a 1.1-litre diesel were also offered, but the article does not
give power or torque figures for either specifically enough to attach to a
trim without guessing, so they are left as a gap rather than approximated
from later Kappa-engine cars.
