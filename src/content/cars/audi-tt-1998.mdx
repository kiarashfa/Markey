The original TT (Typ 8N) shared its A4 (PQ34) platform with the Volkswagen
Golf Mk4 and the first-generation Audi A3, and its curved, minimal bodywork
(inspired, Audi's design team later said, by Bauhaus) was novel enough at
launch to be blamed, rightly or not, for a series of high-speed
accidents.[@wikipedia-audi-tt] The fix was a rear spoiler, revised suspension
and standard stability control, retrofitted across the range from
1999-2000.

Neither the generation infobox nor the detailed per-engine table in this
article gives a kerb weight, an unusual gap for a Wikipedia car article this
thorough. NHTSA's Canadian Vehicle Specifications register fills it for the
1999 model year quattro coupé, and, notably, files the six-cylinder 3.2 VR6
under the very same 1,455 kg entry as the four-cylinder 1.8T, rather than a
separate, heavier figure, which is recorded here rather than assumed to be
wrong.
