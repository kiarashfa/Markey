The F430 replaced the 360 in 2004 and, with it, replaced fifty years of
Dino-derived V8 architecture with an entirely new Ferrari-Maserati engine
family, the F136.[@wikipedia-ferrari-f430] It kept the 360's aluminium chassis
and glasshouse while reworking the body for aerodynamics and adding a
Formula One-style manettino drive-mode selector, a first for a Ferrari road
car.

A single generation, so this entry follows the site's usual one-hub,
one-generation approach and records the launch F430 alongside its lighter,
more powerful 430 Scuderia derivative as trims rather than separate
generations.
