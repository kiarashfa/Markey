LaFerrari's power and torque figures need a careful reading, even though they
pass the site's usual sum-checking trap almost by accident. The V12 makes
800 PS/588 kW on its own; the HY-KERS electric motor adds 163 PS/120 kW; and
the two numbers happen to add up exactly to Ferrari's published combined
total of 963 PS.[@wikipedia-laferrari] That arithmetic coincidence does not
change how the figure is sourced here: 963 PS and 900 N-m are recorded
because Ferrari itself publishes them as the car's combined output, the same
rule this site applies to the Toyota Prius and every other hybrid, not
because this entry added the two component figures together.

Top speed and CO2 are both stated as Ferrari's own claims without an
independent verification given in the article, unlike the car's 0-300 km/h
time (Ferrari's original claim of 15 seconds was later shown to be wrong,
with independent testing finding 21.99 seconds instead). That acceleration
figure is not stored on this entry at all (the schema does not have a
0-300 km/h field), but it is worth noting as a reminder that this article, in
particular, corrects its own manufacturer claim in one place and not others.
