Audi's flagship saloon since 1994, and the car that introduced the
aluminium-bodied Audi Space Frame construction the brand still trades
on.[@wikipedia-audi-a8] It has run through four generations, always with a
long-wheelbase L variant and a high-performance S8, and has offered
front-drive, quattro all-wheel-drive, W12, and — most recently — plug-in
hybrid power.

This entry covers the launch D2 generation and the current D5 generation; the
D3 (2002) and D4 (2009) generations in between are not authored here.
