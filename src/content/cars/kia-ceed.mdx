Kia's European compact hatchback since 2006, the first Kia designed and
built entirely in Europe — styled at Kia's Frankfurt studio and assembled in
Slovakia — and named for the continent it was built for, a lower-case
"cee'd" spelling that Kia itself dropped for the current generation.[@wikipedia-kia-ceed]
It has always shared its platform with the Hyundai i30, sold two generations
in parallel under the "Cee'd"/"Ceed" name across hatchback, estate and
shooting-brake body styles. This catalogue covers the original ED and the
current third-generation CD, on sale since 2018.
