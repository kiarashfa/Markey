The Up was Volkswagen's entry into the A-segment city car class, sold from
2011 to 2023 as a single generation shared, under different badges and with
different faces, as the Škoda Citigo and SEAT Mii.[@wikipedia-volkswagen-up]
It ran the full spread a small hatchback can: a 999 cc three-cylinder base
engine, a proper hot-hatch Up GTI, and (as the e-up!) one of the cheapest
battery-electric cars sold in Europe, twice over two battery generations.
This catalogue records the one generation the Up ever had.
