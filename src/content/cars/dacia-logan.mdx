The car that defined Renault's whole approach to Dacia: developed at
Renault's own Technocentre near Paris and launched in 2004 as a
no-frills saloon built to sell for €5,000, at a time when Renault's own
management considered it "unacceptable" that a good car couldn't be made
that cheaply.[@wikipedia-dacia-logan] It has been built and rebadged
across an unusually wide set of markets and brands — as a Renault, a
Nissan, a Mahindra and, in its estate form, a Lada — and even spawned a
short-lived factory electric conversion in the US. This catalogue covers
the original L90 and the current third-generation LJI.
