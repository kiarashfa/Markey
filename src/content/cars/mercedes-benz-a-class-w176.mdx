The third generation dropped the sandwich floor and the tall, short
proportions that had defined the A-Class since 1997, and repositioned the
car a class up: Mercedes-Benz's own description at launch was the first
"subcompact executive" A-Class, rather than a supermini.[@wikipedia-mercedes-benz-a-class-w176]
It shared its new MFA front-wheel-drive platform with the B-Class, CLA and
GLA that followed it.

At the top of the range sat the A 45 AMG: a turbocharged 2.0-litre four
tuned, in its 2015 facelift form, to 381 PS. At launch, that was the highest specific
output of any four-cylinder production car engine, ahead even of the
Bugatti Veyron's W16 on a per-litre basis. The 4.2-second 0-100 km/h claim
recorded here is specific to cars fitted with the optional Dynamic Plus
package and its mechanical front differential; Mercedes did not separately
publish a time for the standard car.

Length, width and height are all published as ranges across the trim
range rather than one fixed figure. Following this catalogue's rule for a
published range, the lowest value in each is stored, with the full range
given in the note.
