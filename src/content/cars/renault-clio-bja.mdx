Unveiled at the 2019 Geneva Motor Show, the fifth Clio was the first to be
built on the Renault-Nissan CMF-B HS platform, shared with the second
Renault Captur and the Nissan Juke.[@wikipedia-renault-clio] It brought the
Clio its first full hybrid, E-Tech, pairing a 1.6-litre petrol engine with
two electric motors and a 1.2 kWh battery through a clutchless multi-mode
gearbox rather than a conventional transmission.

No kerb weight is recorded for this generation: unlike its predecessors,
Wikipedia's article for the Mk5 gives no generation-level or per-trim mass
figure, and this entry does not carry an invented one. Renault does not sell
the Clio in the US or Canada, so the EPA and NRCan registers this site
otherwise draws on for a missing figure have nothing to offer here either.
