Launched in June 1970 as "A Car For All Reasons": coil springs where every
other off-roader used leaf springs, disc brakes on all four wheels, permanent
four-wheel drive, and a detuned Buick-derived V8.[@wikipedia-range-rover-classic]
Twenty-six years and 326,070 cars later it was still recognisably the same
vehicle.

**This entry has no Test Drive, and the reason is a gap rather than a
decision.** Wikipedia publishes no kerb weight for the Classic in any language,
and no output at all for the 3.9-litre V8. The weight was eventually found
somewhere neither Wikipedia nor this project's source survey had looked:
NHTSA's Canadian Vehicle Specifications, which lists 1,996 kg for the 1994
four-door County Classic.[@nhtsa-cvs-1994-range-rover-county-classic] The power
figure is still missing, and the model cannot run without one.

Everything else is here: the dimensions, the drag coefficient measured on a
1990 car, and the EPA's fuel figure, which works out at
18.1 L/100 km.[@wikipedia-drag-coefficients]

The launch top speed is another honest disagreement. Land Rover claimed around
100 mph; Autocar measured 95 mph in November 1970. Both are recorded.
