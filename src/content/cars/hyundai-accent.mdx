Hyundai's global subcompact since 1994, sold under a shifting set of names —
Excel, Pony and Verna among them — depending on the market and the
year.[@wikipedia-hyundai-accent] This catalogue covers the original X3 of
1994 and the current sixth-generation car, sold from 2023 as the Accent in
most markets and the Verna in India and a handful of others; the four
generations between them are real but thinner in sourceable detail and are
left for a future pass.
