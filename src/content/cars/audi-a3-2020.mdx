The current, fourth-generation A3 launched in 2020 on the MQB evo platform it
shares with the Volkswagen Golf Mk8, SEAT León and Škoda Octavia, styled — the
article says — with visible influence from Lamborghini.[@wikipedia-audi-a3]

One figure here needed a judgement call rather than a lookup. The article
gives the quattro model's output as "190 hp" using a Wikipedia conversion
template whose "hp" unit is mechanical horsepower, not the metric PS used
elsewhere in the same article for other trims. Converting on that literal
reading gives 142 kW; reading it as PS instead — the more common shorthand in
European coverage — would give 140 kW. The gap is small, but the entry records
the reading actually taken and marks the figure `estimated` rather than
`verified`, since the source itself never disambiguates which horsepower unit
its editors meant.

Boot volume also depends on drivetrain: the quattro-equipped car's 320 L is
smaller than the 380 L quoted for front-wheel-drive A3s, because the rear
differential intrudes into the boot floor. Both are manufacturer seats-up
figures; the article separately gives a 1,200 L seats-folded figure that is
not stored here, since the schema's `bootVolume` field is a single seats-up
value.
