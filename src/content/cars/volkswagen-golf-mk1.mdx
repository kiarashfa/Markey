Giugiaro's two-box shape, in production from May 1974, and still being built as
a Cabriolet in 1993, nine years after the hatchback it came from was
replaced.[@wikipedia-volkswagen-golf-mk1] That is why this entry's production
years run to 1993 and why it appears under the 1990s as well as the 1970s.

The [Golf GTI](#gti) is the reason most people remember the car, and it is
also this batch's best illustration of a sourcing problem. Its torque,
acceleration and top speed are all published twice in the same Wikipedia
article, with different figures each time: the prose cites Volkswagen's own
40-years brochure, the model-range table gives something slightly different.
Those three values are marked as having conflicting sources, and both figures
are stated on each. Nothing here silently picks a winner.

Only the Cabriolet carries a drag coefficient, because only the Cabriolet has
one published.[@wikipedia-drag-coefficients] That is also the trim the Test
Drive page models, since it is the one with a complete set of physical inputs.
