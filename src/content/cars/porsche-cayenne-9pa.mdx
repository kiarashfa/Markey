The original Cayenne, on sale from August 2002, met a mixed critical
reception but proved itself with genuinely strong on-road performance and
capable off-road hardware for the class.[@wikipedia-porsche-cayenne] The
range launched with only V8-powered S and Turbo models; a VR6 and a diesel
followed later in the run, and the Turbo S — built to compete with the
Mercedes-Benz ML 63 AMG — arrived in 2006.

Three trims are recorded here: the entry VR6, the naturally aspirated
V8 Cayenne S, and the twin-turbocharged V8 Cayenne Turbo. Peak power for
the Cayenne S is quoted two ways in the source — 340 PS in a specification
table, and a separately cited 335 hp figure attached to a 6.9-second 0-60
mph time. The two power figures agree to within rounding, but the 0-60 mph
time is a different quantity from the 0-100 km/h this catalogue stores, so
it is not converted or recorded here.
