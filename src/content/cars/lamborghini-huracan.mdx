The Huracán replaced the Gallardo in 2014 and ran for a single decade-long
generation, revised in 2019 as the EVO facelift and ending production in 2024
in favour of the hybrid Temerario.[@wikipedia-lamborghini-huracan] Across its
run it was sold in all-wheel-drive and rear-drive form, as a coupé and a
Spyder, and in track-focused special editions culminating in the STO.

See the 2014 entry for the launch LP 610-4 and the final STO that bracket the
range.
