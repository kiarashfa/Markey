The car that created the European MPV: a tall one-box body designed to seat
seven on a car-like platform, rather than a van converted to carry
people.[@wikipedia-renault-espace] Renault took nine sales in its first
month in 1984 before the concept caught on. This entry covers the original,
Matra-built J11 and the fourth-generation J81, the last Espace still sold as
a conventional MPV before the range moved steadily upmarket and, from its
sixth generation in 2023, was rebadged onto a mid-size crossover SUV body —
a car this entry does not cover, since it is no longer the body style the
Espace name made famous.
