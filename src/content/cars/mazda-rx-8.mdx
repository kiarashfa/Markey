The RX-7's replacement solved a problem the RX-7 never tried to: how to fit a
usable back seat around a rotary engine. Mazda's answer was a genuine
four-seat quad coupé with rear-hinged half-doors and no B-pillar, powered by
the Renesis — a side-exhaust-port version of the Wankel engine cleaner enough
to be sold new in markets that had shut the door on rotary power
elsewhere.[@wikipedia-mazda-rx-8] It ran for one generation only, from 2003 to
2012, and Mazda has not sold a rotary-only production car since.
