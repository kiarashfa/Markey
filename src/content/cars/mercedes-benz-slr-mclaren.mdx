A front mid-engined grand tourer built between 2003 and 2010 as a joint
project between Mercedes-Benz, who styled it and owned 40 percent of the
McLaren Group at the time, and McLaren, who engineered, built and tested
every car at their Woking technology centre.[@wikipedia-mercedes-benz-slr-mclaren]
The name is short for "Sport Leicht Rennsport" — sport, light, racing — and
is a direct nod to the 300 SLR racer of 1955.

Only one generation was ever built, so this catalogue's hub-and-generation
structure here holds a single entry: one nameplate, one car, sold as a
coupé, a roadster, and — in its last year — the windscreen-less Stirling
Moss speedster.
