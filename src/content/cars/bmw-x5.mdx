BMW's first SUV, launched in 1999 while the company still owned Land Rover
and shared its hill-descent electronics with the contemporary Range Rover,
built to prove a driver's-car maker could sell a tall all-wheel-drive car
without giving up on-road manners.[@wikipedia-bmw-x5-e53] Four generations
later it is BMW's best-selling model family worldwide, built in South
Carolina for the whole of its life and now offered with everything from a
plug-in-hybrid four-cylinder to a twin-turbo V8 M Competition and a
hydrogen fuel-cell demonstration fleet.
