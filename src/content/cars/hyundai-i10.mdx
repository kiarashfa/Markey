Hyundai's city car since 2007, developed under the codename PA primarily for
the Indian market and replacing the Atos.[@wikipedia-hyundai-i10] It shares
its platform lineage with the Kia Picanto throughout, and the "i10" name has
since covered several genuinely different cars sold in parallel (a
European-built hatchback and a longer, India-built "Grand i10") rather than
one design rolled out worldwide. This catalogue covers the original PA and
the current third-generation car.
