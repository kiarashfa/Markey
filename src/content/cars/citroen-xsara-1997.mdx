Launched in September 1997 on the same platform as the Peugeot 306, the
Xsara replaced the ZX and carried over some of its unusual chassis
tricks — passive rear-wheel steering built into the suspension bushings, and
engines canted rearward in the bay to improve weight distribution.[@wikipedia-citroen-xsara]
A substantial September 2000 facelift touched almost everything: new
sheet metal, a stiffer body, wider tracks, bigger standard wheels, and a
wholesale change of engines, dropping the 1.8-litre range entirely in favour
of new 16-valve 1.6 and 2.0-litre units.

Every power figure recorded here is published directly in kW, so none
needed converting. Kerb weight is a genuine gap: the article states that the
facelift made the car about 80 kg heavier, but gives neither the before nor
the after figure, so the field is left as an explicit placeholder rather
than a guess at either number.
