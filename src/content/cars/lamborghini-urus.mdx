Lamborghini's first SUV since the military LM002, and the model that turned
the company from a niche exotic maker into a volume brand: the Urus regularly
outsells every other Lamborghini combined.[@wikipedia-lamborghini-urus]
Launched in 2017 on Volkswagen Group's MLB Evo platform — shared with the
Audi Q7/Q8, Bentley Bentayga and Porsche Cayenne — it remains in production,
updated in 2024 with a plug-in hybrid powertrain as the Urus SE.
