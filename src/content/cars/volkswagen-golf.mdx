Volkswagen's answer to the question it had been avoiding for a decade: what
replaces the Beetle?[@wikipedia-volkswagen-golf] The answer turned out to be
almost the opposite car (water-cooled, front-engined, front-driven, and shaped
by an Italian), and it has been in continuous production ever since.
