The Hilux began production in March 1968, developed and built for Toyota by
Hino Motors to sit beneath the larger Stout.[@wikipedia-toyota-hilux] Sold in
North America as the Toyota Pickup and then Toyota Truck until the Tacoma
replaced it there in 1995, it has continued everywhere else through eight
generations and a reputation, cultivated largely by amateur destruction
testing, for being nearly impossible to kill.

This entry covers the launch N10 generation and the current eighth
generation (AN110/120/130); the six generations between are not authored
here.
