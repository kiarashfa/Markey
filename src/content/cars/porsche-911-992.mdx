The current 911, in production since 2019 and the first generation to offer
a factory hybrid: the 992.2 Carrera GTS T-Hybrid, launched in 2024, pairs a
turbocharged flat-six with a small electric motor and a 1.9 kWh
battery.[@wikipedia-porsche-911-992] The trim recorded here is the earlier,
purely petrol 992.1 Carrera S: twin-turbocharged, rear-drive, an 8-speed
dual-clutch gearbox as standard, and, with the optional Sport Chrono package,
a 0–100 km/h time under three and a half seconds from a car that still weighs
under 1.5 tonnes.

The dimensions on this entry are stored as the lowest figure Wikipedia gives
across the whole 992 range (narrow-body Carrera up to wide-body Turbo and
GT3) rather than a single trim's exact number, and each field's note says so.
That is a deliberate simplification: this generation now spans a T-Hybrid
GTS, a naturally-aspirated GT3, an off-road Dakar and more variants than one
entry can usefully enumerate.
