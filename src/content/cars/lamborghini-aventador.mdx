Lamborghini's V12 flagship from 2011 to 2022, replacing the Murcielago and,
in turn, replaced by the hybrid Revuelto.[@wikipedia-lamborghini-aventador]
Named, as most Lamborghinis are, after a fighting bull, it kept a naturally
aspirated 6.5-litre V12 and a carbon-fibre monocoque chassis for its entire
eleven-year run, growing in power from 700 PS at launch to 780 PS in the
final Ultimae special edition. This entry treats it as the single generation
it was — Lamborghini never gave the Aventador a numbered second generation —
spanning the launch LP 700-4 through to the SVJ and the closing Ultimae.
