The original Cee'd, the first Kia to be both designed and built entirely in
Europe — styled at Kia's Frankfurt studio and assembled at a new plant in
Žilina, Slovakia.[@wikipedia-kia-ceed] It shared its platform with the
contemporary Hyundai i30, and came in three body styles: the plain five-door
hatchback, a sportier three-door "Pro Cee'd," and an estate, the Cee'd SW.

No power or torque figures for this generation's engines are given in the
Wikipedia article, and the car was never sold in the US, Canada or the
Netherlands, so the EPA, NHTSA and RDW registers this catalogue otherwise
draws on return nothing for it either — a genuine gap rather than an
oversight. Kerb weight is only published as a range across the whole
line-up (1,263-1,572 kg), so the lowest figure is recorded at generation
level rather than assigned to a specific trim.
