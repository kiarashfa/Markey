The second Cayenne, unveiled at the 2010 Geneva Motor Show, kept the first
generation's off-road ability while dropping its low-range transfer case
and shedding around 250 kg through greater use of aluminium and
magnesium.[@wikipedia-porsche-cayenne] Model-year 2013-2016 diesel Cayennes
built on this platform were among the cars implicated in the Volkswagen
Group diesel emissions scandal.

Three trims are recorded here: the entry VR6 petrol, the V6 turbodiesel,
and the twin-turbocharged V8 Cayenne Turbo. The S E-Hybrid plug-in variant
introduced at the 2014 facelift is pictured but not recorded as a separate
trim, since the source does not give it a full power/torque/CO2 figure set
comparable to the other three. Kerb weight is recorded only at generation
level: the article publishes a 2,085-2,215 kg range across the whole line
rather than a figure for any one trim.
