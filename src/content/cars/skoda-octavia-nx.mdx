Revealed in Prague in November 2019 on the Volkswagen Group's MQB Evo
platform, the fourth Octavia was the first Škoda with a head-up display and
the first to offer a plug-in hybrid iV drivetrain alongside its petrol and
diesel range.[@wikipedia-skoda-octavia] It is sold in China in a stretched
form as the Octavia Pro, 64 mm longer with a 44 mm longer wheelbase than
the version sold in Europe: dimensions this entry records only for the
standard European car.

Both trims recorded here give 0-100 km/h directly, so neither figure is
converted from a 0-60 mph time. The plug-in hybrid's power figure is
likewise a single published system output, not a sum of its 150 kW combined
rating and the electric motor's separately quoted 85 kW.
