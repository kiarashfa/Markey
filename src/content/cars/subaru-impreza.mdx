The Impreza has been Subaru's compact car since 1992, built continuously
through six generations and carrying the WRX and WRX STI performance
sub-brands for most of that run before they split off as the standalone
Subaru WRX in 2014.[@wikipedia-subaru-impreza] Every Impreza has offered
Subaru's flat-four "boxer" engine, and all-wheel drive has been standard in
every market except Japan, where it was long optional. This catalogue
covers the original generation (home of the rally-bred WRX STI) and the
current sixth generation, sold only as a hatchback.
