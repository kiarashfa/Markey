Designed years before it reached Renault: British designer Fergus Pollock
sketched the concept for Chrysler UK in the 1970s, Matra developed it for
Chrysler's French subsidiary Simca as a possible Talbot, and only after PSA
judged the project too risky did Matra bring it to Renault as project
J11.[@wikipedia-renault-espace] The result kept Matra's fibreglass-over-
galvanised-steel-spaceframe construction and was built on Matra's own line
in Romorantin, not a Renault plant — a detail that shaped the car's whole
first decade, since Matra's limited capacity kept deliveries constrained
even after demand caught up with the concept.

The English Wikipedia article is unusually thin on hard figures for this
generation: it gives engine displacements but no power, torque or weight for
any of the launch engines. Where noted, this entry draws those figures from
the French-language Wikipedia article's infobox instead — the prose above is
written independently, not translated from it. Even the French article gives
no output figure for the 2.1 Turbo D, so that trim's power field is left as
an honest placeholder rather than a guess.
