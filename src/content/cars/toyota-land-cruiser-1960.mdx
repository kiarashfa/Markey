The J40, launched in 1960 and built for a quarter-century, is the version
most people picture when they hear "Land Cruiser": round headlights, a
removable soft or hard top, and a reputation for running long after everything
around it has failed.[@wikipedia-toyota-land-cruiser] Brazil kept building it,
as the Bandeirante with a Mercedes-Benz diesel engine, until 2001, sixteen
years after Japan stopped.

The F engine's exact displacement is recorded as `estimated` rather than
`verified`: the article calls it simply "3.9 L" without giving a cc figure,
and 3,878 cc, this entry's stated basis, comes from the well-documented
Toyota F engine family rather than a number this specific article states.
