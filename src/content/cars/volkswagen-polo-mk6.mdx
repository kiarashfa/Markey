The current Polo, built on Volkswagen's MQB A0 small-car platform since
2017 and shared underneath with the SEAT Ibiza, Škoda Fabia and Audi
A1.[@wikipedia-volkswagen-polo-mk6] It grew again over its predecessor, as
every Polo generation has, and now sits close to where the original Golf
was sized.

The GTI's torque is a deliberate gap: the article states its 200 PS power
figure clearly but never gives a torque figure anywhere in the text, so none
is recorded here rather than carried over from an earlier Polo GTI
generation's number.
