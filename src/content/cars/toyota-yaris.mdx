A small hatchback with an unusually large number of names: the same car has
been sold as the Yaris, the Vitz, the Echo, the Platz and the Belta,
depending on market and year.[@wikipedia-toyota-yaris] It replaced the
Starlet and Tercel in 1999 and has spent every generation since as Toyota's
entry-level European and Japanese supermini, closing the B-segment gap in
this catalogue.
