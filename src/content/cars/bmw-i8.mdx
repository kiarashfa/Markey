BMW's answer to the question of what a plug-in hybrid sports car could be
rather than merely an efficient one: a carbon-fibre passenger cell, a
three-cylinder petrol engine driving the rear wheels, and an electric motor
driving the front, styled almost unchanged from the 2009 Vision
EfficientDynamics concept.[@wikipedia-bmw-i8] It sold for one generation,
2014 to 2020, as a coupé and later a roadster, and BMW has not built a direct
successor.
