Chevrolet's answer to the Ford Mustang, rushed to market on a new F-body
platform two and a half years after the Mustang had proven there was a market
for an affordable, sporty two-door built on humble mechanicals.[@wikipedia-chevrolet-camaro-first-generation]
Six generations ran from 1966 to 2023, swinging between compact pony car and
outright muscle car depending on the decade, before Chevrolet ended production
in December 2023 with no direct successor announced.
