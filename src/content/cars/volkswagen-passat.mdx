Volkswagen's mid-size family car, in production since 1973 and sold in
North America for most of its early life as the Dasher.[@wikipedia-volkswagen-passat]
It shared its first-generation platform with the Audi 80, a pattern of
badge-engineered sharing between the two marques that has continued in one
form or another ever since.
