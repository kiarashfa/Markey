Citroën's C-segment family car since 2004, launched as a five-door hatchback
and three-door coupe sharing its floor with the Peugeot 307.[@wikipedia-citroen-c4]
The name has covered a genuinely wide range of body styles over three
generations: coupe, saloon, MPV (as the related C4 Picasso), and, since
2020, a "coupe SUV" shape sold alongside a fastback saloon twin, the C4 X.
This catalogue covers the original B5 and the current third-generation C41.
