Mercedes-Benz's compact executive saloon, sold since 1993 as the replacement
for the 190 and the entry point into the three-pointed star.[@wikipedia-mercedes-benz-c-class]
Six generations have followed, each keeping the same formula: a smaller,
cheaper E-Class, engineered to the same standard rather than a diluted one.

This entry covers two of them: the original W202 of 1993, the car that
introduced the C-Class name and its first AMG performance model, and the
third-generation W204 of 2007, the version that finally brought a proper
naturally aspirated V8 AMG saloon within reach of an E46 M3's price. The two
generations in between (W203 and W205) and the current one are gaps in this
catalogue, not omissions from the car's history.
