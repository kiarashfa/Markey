Developed from 1989 under the internal code 153T and launched in Japan in
May 1994, the XA10 offered three- and five-door bodies on different
wheelbases, plus a rare soft-top convertible three-door — body styles no
RAV4 since has repeated.[@wikipedia-toyota-rav4] It reached the US in
January 1996 and was named Automobile Magazine's 1997 Automobile of the
Year. A separate battery-electric RAV4 EV, leased and briefly sold in
California from 1997, is not authored as a trim here, since this entry
covers the petrol range only.

Wikipedia's own article gives no kerb weight for this generation, so this
entry's mass and weight-distribution figures instead come from NHTSA's
Canadian Vehicle Specifications register, matched to the closest available
body/drivetrain combination (5-door AWD) rather than the base US 2.0 trim
exactly as sold.
