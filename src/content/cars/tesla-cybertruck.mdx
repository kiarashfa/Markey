Tesla's electric pickup, unveiled as a concept in 2019 and delivered from
November 2023 after repeated delays, built around unpainted stainless-steel
body panels instead of a conventional painted skin.[@wikipedia-tesla-cybertruck]
It has sold in far smaller numbers than Tesla originally projected and has
drawn sustained criticism for build quality and its styling, but it remains
the highest-profile attempt yet to sell a full-size electric pickup outside
Ford and GM's own trucks. This entry, as with any single-generation
nameplate, covers one generation entry spanning the whole production run so
far.
