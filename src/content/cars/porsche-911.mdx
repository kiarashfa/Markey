A rear-engined, air-cooled sports car launched in 1963 as a replacement for
the 356, and never actually replaced since — every 911 built in the sixty
years after it, water-cooled or not, has kept the same basic layout and the
same name.[@wikipedia-porsche-911-classic] Markey covers two of its eight
generations: the original air-cooled car of 1964–1989, which established the
shape, the flat-six layout and the Targa roof; and the current 992, the
seventh distinct generation, still recognisably the same idea with a
twin-turbo flat-six and, on some models, mild-hybrid assistance. The five
generations between them — 964, 993, 996, 997 and 991 — are not yet catalogued
here.
