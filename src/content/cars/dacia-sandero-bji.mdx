The current Sandero, launched alongside the Logan III in September 2020 on
the shared Renault-Nissan CMF-B platform, and sold in Italy, Greece and
Morocco as the Sandero Streetway.[@wikipedia-dacia-sandero] Unlike its Logan
saloon sibling, Wikipedia's Sandero article carries a full, directly
sourced power and torque table for this generation, which is why this entry
is fuller than the equivalent Logan III page despite the two sharing
mechanicals.

The bi-fuel ECO-G 100 records its LPG-mode output as the trim's headline
figure, matching its `lpg` powertrain classification; a newer 1.2-litre
ECO-G 120 engine, added at the 2025 facelift with different figures in
petrol and LPG mode, is left for a future pass rather than folded into this
trim. Kerb weight is left as an explicit placeholder, as it is for the
mechanically related Logan III — the infobox field is present but empty.
