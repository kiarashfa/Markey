Citroën's supermini since 2002, sharing its underpinnings with the Peugeot
1007 and 206/207 in its first generation and, by its third, splitting into
two genuinely unrelated cars sold in parallel: a European hatchback and a
"completely unrelated," in Wikipedia's own words, model built on a different
platform for India and Latin America.[@wikipedia-citroen-c3] Confusingly,
Citroën and Wikipedia both call that India/Latin America car part of the
"third generation," while the reskinned European version that followed it
in 2024 is labelled a distinct "fourth generation" despite sharing its
platform and most of its mechanicals. This catalogue covers three points in
that story: the original FC/FN, the European third-generation SX/SY
(2016-2024), and the current fourth-generation CC21, on sale in Europe
since 2024.
