The last Fiesta, built at Cologne from 2017 until Ford ended production of
the entire nameplate in July 2023 to concentrate on SUVs and electric
models.[@wikipedia-ford-fiesta-seventh-generation] Wikipedia's own count
calls this the "seventh generation"; most enthusiast and trade usage in the
UK calls it the Mk8, because Ford treats the 2008 and 2013 cars as one
generation with a facelift where Wikipedia counts them as two — this entry
follows Wikipedia's numbering and notes the discrepancy rather than picking
a side.

The ST hot-hatch trim recorded here carries the pre-facelift figures
(2018-2022); Ford revised the engine's torque and CO2 figures at the 2022
facelift, and the two are kept as distinct concerns rather than blended into
one figure.
