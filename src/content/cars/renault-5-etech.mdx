Grown from a designer's unauthorised mockup shown to Renault's CEO in 2020,
the E-Tech revives the 5 name on a battery-electric hatchback built on the
same AmpR Small platform as the Renault 4 E-Tech and the Alpine
A290.[@wikipedia-renault-5-e-tech] It won European Car of the Year in 2025
and quickly became one of the best-selling electric cars in Europe, with the
large majority of its buyers new to the Renault brand.

Two motor and battery combinations are recorded here directly from the
infobox's confirmed production specification. Electric range is left as an
explicit placeholder rather than filled with the "expected 400 km" figure
quoted by a Renault executive before the car's final specification was
set — a pre-launch estimate, not a confirmed WLTP number, and the source
does not give one.
