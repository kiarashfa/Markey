The third-generation C3 for Europe, revealed in June 2016 with styling cues
borrowed from the facelifted C4 Picasso and optional "Airbump" side
mouldings shared with the C4 Cactus.[@wikipedia-citroen-c3] It ran for eight
years, longer than either generation before it, before Citroën replaced it
in Europe with a reskinned version of the unrelated Indian-market car rather
than a clean-sheet design.

Three trims are recorded here, spanning the two petrol PureTech states of
tune and the diesel that eventually replaced an earlier, larger-displacement
unit. All are drawn from the manufacturer's own published specification
table rather than a prose estimate.
