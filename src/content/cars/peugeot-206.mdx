Peugeot's best-selling model ever, with more than 8.3 million sold
worldwide by 2012, and Autocar's thirtieth-longest-lived single-generation
car when it counted in 2020.[@wikipedia-peugeot-206] It replaced the 205 in
1998 and, in one market or another, was still being built and sold (as the
206+, the 207 Compact, the 207i or the 206 SD) for over two decades after
its original launch.

This entry covers the original European-market T1 generation, from its 1998
launch to the end of French production in 2012. The many rebadged and
facelifted continuations sold elsewhere (Brazil, Iran, China and Latin
America chief among them) are gaps in this catalogue, not omissions from
the car's history.
