The original A8 (Typ 4D) introduced the Audi Space Frame — an aluminium
unibody rather than steel — to a full-size saloon, at a time when the
technology was still novel enough that period coverage dwelt on it at
length.[@wikipedia-audi-a8] It replaced the Audi V8 and, unusually for the
class, offered quattro all-wheel drive across most of its engine range rather
than as an occasional option.

This is one of relatively few generations on this site where the source
publishes weight per individual engine and drivetrain combination rather than
one figure for the whole range — thirteen distinct weights in total, from a
1,540 kg base 2.8 to a 1,980 kg long-wheelbase W12. That precision let both
trims recorded here take an exact, engine-matched kerb weight rather than an
estimate.
