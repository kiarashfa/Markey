Lamborghini's first V10 and its first genuine volume model, the Gallardo ran
for a single ten-year generation from 2003 to 2013 and became the
best-selling Lamborghini of its time, with 14,022 built.[@wikipedia-lamborghini-gallardo]
It sat alongside the V12 Murciélago as the smaller, cheaper, all-wheel-drive
entry point to the range, and was replaced by the Huracán in 2014.

See the 2003 entry for the base car and the LP 560-4 facelift that make up
its production run.
