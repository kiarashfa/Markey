The original C4, unveiled at the 2004 Paris Motor Show as a five-door
hatchback and a distinct three-door coupe with its own rear-end
design.[@wikipedia-citroen-c4] A saloon version, built in Argentina and
exported as the C4 Pallas or C4 Sedan, and a longer four-door export to
China badged the C-Triomphe, extended the same platform much further than
the European car alone suggests.

Four trims are recorded here, each with kerb weight, performance and
economy figures pulled directly from the manufacturer's own specification
table rather than a single generation-wide estimate — the table gives a
different weight for nearly every engine and body combination, so no single
figure would have described the range. The saloon's separately quoted drag
coefficient of 0.28 is recorded on the diesel trim as an estimate, since it
describes the saloon body specifically and not the hatchback/coupe shape
those figures are otherwise drawn from.
