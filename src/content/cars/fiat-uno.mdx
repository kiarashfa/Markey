The Uno name covers two cars that never overlapped in any market and share
almost no parts: the boxy, Giugiaro-styled European supermini that won Car of
the Year in 1984 and sold across Europe, Poland and Yugoslavia through 1995,
and an unrelated Brazilian city car revived under the same name in 2010 after
Fiat retired the "Mille" it had been calling its Brazilian-built Uno
descendant since 1990.[@wikipedia-fiat-uno] Between them, the Uno name covers
more than 8.8 million cars: the eighth most-produced car platform in
history counting the original alone, and considerably more with the Brazilian
cars added in.
