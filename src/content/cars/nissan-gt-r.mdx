The car Nissan built to retire the Skyline GT-R name entirely: a
clean-sheet, all-wheel-drive coupé sold worldwide rather than in the limited
markets its predecessors reached, and developed under chief engineer
Kazutoshi Mizuno specifically to beat a Porsche 911 Turbo's Nürburgring lap
time.[@wikipedia-nissan-gt-r] One generation, the R35, ran for eighteen years
without ever being replaced — power rose from 480 PS at launch to 600 PS in
the Nismo before production finally ended in August 2025.
