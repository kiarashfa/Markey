Audi's mid-size executive saloon, sold under that name since 1994 but really
the fourth generation of a lineage that started as the Audi
100.[@wikipedia-audi-a6] The 1994 rename was cosmetic — a facelifted Audi 100
(C4) given new lights, a new grille and Audi's new alphanumeric badge, timed
to match the launch of the flagship A8. Markey covers that first badged
generation, the C4, and the fifth, the C8 of 2018 — the most recent complete
generation catalogued on Wikipedia at the time of writing, with the C9 that
followed it in 2025 not yet built out here.
