Launched in Japan in June 2001, replacing the Honda Logo, the first Fit
built its whole interior packaging around a fuel tank moved from the usual
rear-axle location to under the front seats, freeing a completely flat
floor and letting the "Ultra Seat" fold in configurations no rival hatchback
of the size could match.[@wikipedia-honda-fit-first-generation] It didn't
reach North America until 2006, five years after its Japanese launch, sold
there with a single 1.5-litre VTEC engine rather than the four petrol
options offered elsewhere.

Honda's Brazilian market also received a flex-fuel version running on any
mix of petrol and ethanol, rated slightly differently on each fuel (80 hp on
petrol, 83 hp on ethanol), a real difference in the fuel's energy content,
not a rounding inconsistency. It is not recorded as a trim here since this
entry focuses on the US-market car.
