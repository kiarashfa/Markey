Revealed as a production car at the 2024 Paris Motor Show and on sale from
April 2025, the 4 E-Tech borrows nothing mechanical from the original
car (it shares its AmpR Small platform, battery and motor with the Renault
5 E-Tech and Alpine A290) and revives the name alone, on a battery-electric
crossover body designed by Gilles Vidal.[@wikipedia-renault-4-e-tech]

Wikipedia's own article on the car is tagged by its editors as needing an
update, and it shows: the only motor output and battery capacity given
anywhere in the source describe the 2022 4Ever Trophy show car, not the
production model that actually went on sale, so both are recorded here as
placeholders rather than borrowed from a different vehicle. Euro NCAP's
test report separately names a production trim "EV52 Techno," which implies
at least one real battery size exists, but without a figure attached to it.
