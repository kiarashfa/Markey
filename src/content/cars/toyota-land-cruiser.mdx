The Land Cruiser traces to the Toyota Jeep BJ of 1951 (a body-on-frame
off-roader developed to compete with the Jeep-derived vehicles the Japanese
police were then buying from Mitsubishi) and was renamed Land Cruiser in
1954.[@wikipedia-toyota-land-cruiser] Wikipedia's lineage for the nameplate
runs through more than a dozen series across seven decades, split between
Heavy Duty off-road-oriented models and more comfort-oriented Station Wagon
models.

This entry covers two of that long lineage: the classic J40 (the FJ40 and its
Brazilian-built Bandeirante derivative, 1960-1985) and the current J300
(2021-present). Everything between (J50, J60, J70, J80, J100 and J200 among
others) is not authored here.
