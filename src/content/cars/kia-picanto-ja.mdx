The third and current Picanto, unveiled at the 2017 Geneva Motor Show and
sold in South Korea and several other markets as the Morning, a name it has
carried since the very first generation.[@wikipedia-kia-picanto] A factory
LPG version of the 1.0-litre engine remains in the range, as it was on the
original car.

This generation is a genuine sourcing gap rather than a thin entry: the
Wikipedia article lists its three petrol engines but gives no power, torque
or economy figures for any of them, and the car is not sold in the US,
Canada or the Netherlands, so the EPA, NHTSA and RDW registers that fill
gaps elsewhere in this catalogue return nothing for it either. Kerb weight is
left as an explicit placeholder for the same reason: the infobox field is
present but empty.
