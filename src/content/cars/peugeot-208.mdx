The 208 has been Peugeot's supermini since 2012, succeeding the 207 and, in
its current second generation, offering petrol, diesel and battery-electric
versions of the same body and platform.[@wikipedia-peugeot-208] The first
generation carried a GTi hot-hatch flagship, badge-engineered from a
turbocharged 1.6-litre engine; Peugeot deliberately let the GTi lapse for the
second generation because the old engine could not meet CO2 targets, and has
since revealed an e-208 GTi built around the electric drivetrain instead.
This catalogue covers both generations.
