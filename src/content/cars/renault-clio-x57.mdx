The original Clio, launched at the 1990 Paris Motor Show to replace the
Renault 5, though it shared the 5's floorpan and suspension design rather
than starting fresh.[@wikipedia-renault-clio] It won European Car of the
Year for 1991 and, in the UK, became a fixture of television advertising
through the "Papa and Nicole" campaign.

The car sold here as an everyday hatchback was joined in 1993 by the Clio
Williams, a homologation special named for the Renault-powered Williams
Formula One team, which had no hand in designing it; the work was Renault
Sport's. Renault built far more Williamses than the 3,800 homologation
requirement, then kept building "Williams 2" and "Williams 3" editions to
meet demand, to the annoyance of early buyers promised exclusivity. Kerb
weight is recorded here only for the base range: Wikipedia's infobox gives a
single figure for the whole generation, and no separate weight is published
for the heavier 16V or Williams.
