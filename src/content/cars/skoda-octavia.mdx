Škoda's best-selling nameplate and the car that re-established the brand
in Western Europe after Volkswagen took control in 1991: the first
genuinely modern Škoda, sharing its platform and engines with the
contemporary Volkswagen Golf and Audi A3 rather than anything from the
communist-era range it replaced.[@wikipedia-skoda-octavia] The name itself
is a revival — Škoda had used "Octavia" for a car in the 1960s — chosen to
signal a fresh start rather than a continuation. This entry covers the
original Mk1 and the current Mk4.
