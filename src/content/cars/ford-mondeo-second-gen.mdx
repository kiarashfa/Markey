The second Mondeo, on sale from November 2000, shared its platform with the
Jaguar X-Type and moved to Mazda-derived four-cylinder engines, branded
Duratec by Ford, in place of the outgoing Zetec range.[@wikipedia-ford-mondeo-second-generation]
Sales peaked above 100,000 units a year in the UK during the 1990s and
still topped 80,000 in 2001, a scale later generations never matched.

Three trims are recorded here: the 2.0-litre petrol, the 2.0 TDCi diesel,
and the ST220 — a newly developed 3.0-litre V6 replacing the previous
generation's ST200, sold as a saloon, liftback and estate. As with the
first generation, the ST220's published 0-62 mph time is not converted to
a 0-100 km/h figure, since the two describe different speed thresholds;
only its top speed, a straightforward unit conversion, is recorded.
