Volkswagen's supermini, sold below the Golf since 1975 and, for its first
generation, essentially a rebadged Audi 50 with Bertone
styling.[@wikipedia-volkswagen-polo] It has run for six generations without
a gap, closing the B-segment alongside the Fiesta, Yaris and Clio in this
catalogue.
