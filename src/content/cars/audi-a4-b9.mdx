The last Audi A4: on sale from 2015 to 2025, when the saloon was renamed A5
for its successor as Audi consolidated its odd- and even-numbered model
lines.[@wikipedia-audi-a4] It arrived roughly 120 kg lighter than the B8 it
replaced thanks to an aluminium-hybrid structure, and it was the first A4 with
a fully digital "virtual cockpit" instrument display, Apple CarPlay and
Android Auto.

The trim recorded here is the 40 TFSI quattro — badged 2.0 TFSI ultra at
launch — a mainstream all-wheel-drive four-cylinder rather than the base
front-drive engine or the S4/RS4 performance flagships also sold on this
platform. Its spec table in the article also gives combined fuel economy and
CO₂ figures, but without naming the test cycle they were measured on; since a
2015-launch European car could plausibly be either the outgoing NEDC cycle
or an early WLTP figure, and the two are not comparable, both fields are
left out rather than guessed at.
