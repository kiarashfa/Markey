America's one continuously produced sports car: a front-engine, rear-drive
roadster in 1953 that GM's own management nearly cancelled after two slow
years, kept alive by a V8 engine, and eventually turned, seven generations
and 67 years later, into a mid-engined car built to fight Ferraris and
Porsches on price.[@wikipedia-chevrolet-corvette-c1] It has never been sold
as anything other than a Chevrolet, and never stopped being built in the
United States.
