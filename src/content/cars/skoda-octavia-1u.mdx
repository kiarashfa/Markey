Launched in November 1996 and built on the Volkswagen Group's A4 (PQ34)
platform, shared with the Golf Mk4 and Audi A3, the Mk1 Octavia was the car
that proved a Volkswagen-owned Škoda could compete on genuinely equal
terms.[@wikipedia-skoda-octavia] It briefly launched with a very basic
1.4-litre, 60 PS engine descended from the 1964 Škoda 1000 MB: a leftover
from the pre-Volkswagen era that proved underpowered and was dropped within
two years for a proper Volkswagen 1.4.

No kerb weight is recorded here: Wikipedia's infobox leaves the field
blank for this generation, and the Netherlands RDW register this site
