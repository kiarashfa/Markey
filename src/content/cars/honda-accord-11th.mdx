Unveiled in November 2022 on Honda's new HA platform, the current Accord
dropped the previous generation's optional 2.0-litre turbo and coupe body
entirely, settling on a single 1.5-litre turbo four or a hybrid, sold only as
a four-door saloon.[@wikipedia-honda-accord] It grew in every dimension over
its predecessor to make room for the hybrid system's battery, and Honda
expected — correctly, going by 2024 US sales — that the hybrid would account
for half the car's sales going in.

No combined system output is recorded for the hybrid (CY2) here: Honda
publishes the petrol engine and the electric motor's figures for the
previous generation's version of this powertrain, but not a combined system
number for this generation, and this site does not sum hybrid outputs
that were never published as one figure.
