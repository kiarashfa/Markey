The second Focus, launched at the 2004 Paris Motor Show, kept its
predecessor's suspension architecture largely unchanged but grew in every
dimension — 25 mm more wheelbase, and increases in length, width and
height that Ford said improved ride at some cost to the sharper handling
enthusiasts associated with the Mk1.[@wikipedia-ford-focus-second-generation-europe]
A two-door coupé-cabriolet with a retractable hardtop, built by Pininfarina
in Turin, joined the range in 2007.

Three trims are recorded here: the 1.6 Duratec petrol, the 2.0 TDCi
diesel, and the RS of 2009 — Ford's first Focus RS with the previous
generation's inline-four dropped for a turbocharged, Volvo-derived
straight-five shared with the Focus ST, driving the front wheels through a
new "RevoKnuckle" suspension designed to fight torque steer. As with the
first generation, two engine displacements here (the 1.6 petrol and 2.0
diesel) are recorded as `estimated`, since the source states only the
rounded litre figure for each rather than an exact cubic-centimetre value.
