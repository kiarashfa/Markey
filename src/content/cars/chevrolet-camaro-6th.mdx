The last Camaro, built on GM's Alpha platform and offered with everything
from a turbocharged four-cylinder to a supercharged 6.2-litre V8 shared with
the contemporary Corvette Z06.[@wikipedia-chevrolet-camaro-sixth-generation]
The SS's naturally aspirated LT1 and the ZL1's supercharged LT4 are both
Corvette engines wearing Camaro badges — the SS shares its unit with the C7
Corvette, the ZL1 with the C7 Z06 — carried over rather than developed
specifically for this car.

Production ended in December 2023 without a direct replacement, closing out
57 years of Camaro production; Chevrolet has floated an electric successor
but had not put one into production as of this entry's last verification.
