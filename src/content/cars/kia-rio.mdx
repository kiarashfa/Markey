Kia's subcompact since 1999, launched using Mazda engines under a technical
partnership that predated Kia's own Hyundai-derived powertrains, and sold at
launch as the least expensive mass-produced car on the US
market.[@wikipedia-kia-rio] The nameplate has since split into genuinely
different cars built for different regions: a global version built in
Korea and Mexico, and a longer-wheelbase Russian/Chinese version sold as the
Kia K2. They share a name more than a design. This catalogue covers the
original DC and the fourth-generation global YB, whose production ended in
December 2023.
