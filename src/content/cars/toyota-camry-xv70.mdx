The current Camry, launched in 2017 on Toyota's TNGA-K platform and sold as
a saloon only: the wagon and coupé variants that earlier generations
carried are gone.[@wikipedia-toyota-camry-xv70] It is built in the US at
Georgetown, Kentucky, a plant that has made Camrys since the 1980s.

The hybrid's power figure is this entry's one flagged conflict: Wikipedia's
own article gives two different combined outputs for the same US-market
Hybrid LE: 208 hp in the prose, citing a Car and Driver road test, and
131 kW (176 hp) in the article's own trim table, with no explanation for the
gap. That is more than 15% apart and reads as an error in one or the other,
not two different markets, so it is recorded here as a flagged conflict
rather than silently picking one.
