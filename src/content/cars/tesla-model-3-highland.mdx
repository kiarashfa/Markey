The 2023 facelift, known inside the company as Highland: new lamps, a smoother
nose, and a claimed improvement in drag.[@wikipedia-tesla-model-3]

That claim is exactly why this entry carries no drag coefficient. The figure
in the aerodynamics list is for the 2017 car, and the facelift changed the nose
specifically to beat it, so copying the old number across would describe a car
that no longer exists.
