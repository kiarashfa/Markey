The Celica ran for thirty-six years and seven generations, from a 1970
coupé built to answer the Ford Mustang to a 2006 front-wheel-drive liftback
sold as part of Toyota's failed Project Genesis push for younger US
buyers.[@wikipedia-toyota-celica] It spawned the Celica Supra/Celica XX, which
split off as its own Supra nameplate in 1986, and a rally-bred GT-Four
all-wheel-drive line that ended with the fifth generation.

This entry covers the launch A20/A30 generation and the final T230
generation; the five generations between are not authored here.
