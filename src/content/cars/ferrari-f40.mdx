Built to mark Ferrari's fortieth anniversary and the last car Enzo Ferrari
personally signed off before his death in 1988, the F40 was a road-legal
version of the abandoned 288 GTO Evoluzione Group B programme, stripped
back rather than dressed up.[@wikipedia-ferrari-f40] No carpets, no radio,
no power steering, and door panels that were just a fabric pull-cord over
bare composite. This entry covers the single road-going generation,
1987-1992; the LM, Competizione and GTE competition derivatives that
continued in small numbers to 1996 are not authored as separate trims here.
