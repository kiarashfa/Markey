The car that put the world on wheels: over 15 million built in nineteen
years, the first car most American families could actually
afford.[@wikipedia-ford-model-t] Henry Ford's brief was a car "large enough
for the family, but small enough for the individual to run and care for" —
and the moving assembly line that built it, more than the car itself, is
what changed the twentieth century.
