The current Passat, and the first generation sold in Europe as an estate
only — Volkswagen dropped the saloon body from the mainstream European range
entirely, leaving the Škoda Superb to cover that shape within the
group.[@wikipedia-volkswagen-passat-b9] A saloon derivative continues in
China as the separately badged Passat Pro, which this entry does not cover.

Every non-hybrid petrol and diesel engine in this generation is paired only
with a dual-clutch automatic; unlike its predecessor, no manual gearbox is
offered at all.
