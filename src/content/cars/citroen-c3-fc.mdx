The original C3, launched in January 2002 as a five-door hatchback and
styled under Donato Coco, who had also drawn the Xsara Picasso.[@wikipedia-citroen-c3]
Under PSA's platform-sharing policy it used the same chassis as the Peugeot
1007 and much of the same componentry as the Peugeot 206. A quirky
convertible derivative, the C3 Pluriel, offered five different open-top
configurations from a fixed roof to a full roadster pickup bed — Top Gear
later named it one of the worst cars of the previous twenty years for how
awkwardly that roof worked in practice.

Four engines are recorded here from the seven or eight the car was
eventually sold with; all of their outputs are published directly in kW in
the source, so no unit conversion was needed for any of them. Kerb weight is
only published as a range across the whole line-up, so the lowest figure is
recorded at generation level rather than assigned to a specific trim.
