A single-generation nameplate: the Xsara ran from 1997 to 2006, replacing
the ZX and sharing its platform with the Peugeot 306, before being
succeeded in Europe by the C4.[@wikipedia-citroen-xsara] The hatchback ended
production in Europe in 2004, but a Chinese joint-venture plant kept
building it for another year, and the estate outlasted the hatchback
everywhere, running to 2006 without ever getting a direct replacement. This
catalogue treats the whole run as one entry, with the substantial 2000
facelift and its many engine changes recorded as trims.
