A body-on-frame off-roader proposed to Mercedes-Benz by the Shah of Iran, built
from 1979 as a military and government vehicle, and sold alongside its own
luxury reinvention for most of this century.[@wikipedia-mercedes-benz-g-class]
Wikipedia's own article treats the whole run under three chassis codes (W460,
W461 and W463) with W461 remaining in military and utility production
essentially unchanged while W463 became progressively more luxurious around
it. Markey covers the two W463 generations: the original 1990–2018 car that
first put a G-Class next to an S-Class in the showroom, and the ground-up
2018 redesign that kept the same boxy silhouette and the same chassis code
despite almost nothing underneath carrying over.
