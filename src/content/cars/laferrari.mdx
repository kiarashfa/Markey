Ferrari's first full-hybrid road car and, at launch, the most powerful road
car the company had built, LaFerrari paired an 800 PS naturally aspirated V12
with a KERS-derived electric motor for a combined 963 PS.[@wikipedia-laferrari]
It replaced the Enzo as the marque's halo car in 2013 and was itself replaced
by the F80.

A single generation, sold as a fixed-roof coupé from 2013 and, from 2016, as
the targa-top Aperta.
