The sixth car formally sold as an E-Class, on sale since May 2023, and the
first generation where every engine in the range carries some form of
electrification — 48-volt mild-hybrid assistance as standard, with plug-in
hybrid petrol and diesel options offering a 25.4 kWh battery
alongside.[@wikipedia-mercedes-benz-e-class-w214] The entry-level E 200
recorded here is a conventional turbocharged four-cylinder helped by a small
integrated starter-generator, not a full hybrid: the combustion engine's 204
PS and the starter-generator's separately-rated 23 PS are not combined into
one system figure by the source, so they are not combined here either.

The dimensions on this entry are the lowest figure Wikipedia's infobox gives
across the whole line — narrow four-cylinder saloon to the heaviest plug-in
hybrid estate — each field's note says so, rather than pretending one number
describes every W214 on sale.
