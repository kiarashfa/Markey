The 307 replaced the well-regarded 306 in 2001 and stayed in production in
Europe until 2008, when the 308 took over the hatchback, estate and coupé-
cabriolet body styles while a new saloon line went to the 408.[@wikipedia-peugeot-307]
Outside Europe it lasted much longer: production continued in China until
2014 and in Argentina until 2011. A rally version, the Peugeot 307 WRC, won
races for the factory team, and Peugeot built an SW estate with an optional
third-row seat instead of a dedicated compact MPV, unlike Citroën's
contemporary Xsara Picasso. This catalogue records the one generation the
307 had in its home market.
