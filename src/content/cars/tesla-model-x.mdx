Tesla's first SUV, built on a stretched and raised version of the Model S
platform and known chiefly for its rear falcon-wing doors.[@wikipedia-tesla-model-x]
It ran for over a decade on one generation, revised in 2021 with a new
interior, powertrain and the tri-motor "Plaid" performance flagship, before
Tesla discontinued it alongside the Model S in early 2026.

A single generation, recorded here as the current dual-motor Long Range and
tri-motor Plaid; the discontinued 60D/75D/90D/100D/P90D/P100D line that
preceded the 2021 refresh is not broken out as separate trims.
