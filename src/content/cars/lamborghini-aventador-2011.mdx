Launched at the 2011 Geneva Motor Show as the LP 700-4, the Aventador
replaced the decade-old Murcielago with an all-new carbon-fibre monocoque, a
pushrod inboard suspension, and Lamborghini's L539 V12, its fifth in-house
engine design and only its second all-new V12.[@wikipedia-lamborghini-aventador]
Every subsequent variant, from the lighter SuperVeloce through the S facelift
to the track-honed SVJ, kept that same basic engine and chassis architecture,
tuning power upward at each step; the closing Ultimae edition, limited to
600 cars, made 780 PS, eighty more than the original launch car.

Wikipedia's own specification table publishes 0-100 km/h acceleration
directly for every trim, which this entry uses as given; none of the
figures here are converted from a 0-60 mph time, the trap that catches a
lot of American-market performance data. Where a trim's kerb weight is
published as a range across coupe and roadster body styles (the SVJ), the
lower, coupe-body figure is recorded, per the site's convention for stored
ranges.
