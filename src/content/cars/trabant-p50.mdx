Introduced in 1958 as the P 50, this is the car everything that followed was
a facelift of: a duroplast-bodied, front-wheel-drive small car with a
transverse two-stroke twin descended from a pre-war DKW design.[@wikipedia-trabant]
Power grew twice in six years without any real change to the car underneath
it — 17 PS at launch, 20 PS in 1960 with a newly synchronised gearbox, and 23
PS in 1962 when the engine grew to 594 cc and the car was renamed P 60 ahead
of the 601 facelift.

Only running-change output figures are recorded here as trims; no acceleration
or dimensional figures specific to the P50/P60 period, as distinct from the
601 that followed it, are given anywhere in the source.
