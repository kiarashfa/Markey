The car that created the pony car class: a sporty, affordable coupé built on
mundane Falcon underpinnings, sold from a standing start in April 1964 to
over a million units within its first eighteen months.[@wikipedia-ford-mustang]
It has stayed in continuous production ever since, the only pony car of its
generation never to leave the market entirely.
