Opel's compact-class nameplate since 1991, replacing the Kadett and taking a
name Vauxhall had already used in Britain for two Kadett generations
sold as the "Vauxhall Astra".[@wikipedia-opel-astra] It has been sold
under half a dozen badges around the world — Vauxhall, Holden, Chevrolet
and, briefly, Saturn in North America — and is now into its sixth
generation. This entry covers three: the original Astra F, the Astra H that
carried the nameplate's best-known hot-hatch variant and its only trip to
the US market, and the current Astra L.
