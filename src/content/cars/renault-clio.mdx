Renault's supermini, on sale continuously since 1990 as the replacement for
the Renault 5, and consistently one of Europe's best-selling
cars.[@wikipedia-renault-clio] It is one of only two nameplates — the other
being the Volkswagen Golf — to have won European Car of the Year twice, in
1991 and 2006. This entry covers three of its six generations: the original
X57, the volume-selling X65 that carried both the everyday hatchback and the
Renault Sport performance line, and the current BJA.
