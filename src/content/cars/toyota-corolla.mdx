The best-selling nameplate in automotive history, and one of the least
dramatic: the Corolla's whole reputation rests on doing the ordinary things
(starting, running to schedule, holding its value) without
incident.[@wikipedia-toyota-corolla] It has been built continuously since
1966, through twelve generations and a shift from rear-wheel to front-wheel
drive, without ever chasing segment leadership on power or style.
