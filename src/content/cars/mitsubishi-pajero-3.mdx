The third Pajero, launched in Japan in August 1999 and elsewhere as a 2001
model, replaced the earlier body-on-frame construction with a unibody over
an integrated ladder frame (Mitsubishi's RISE reinforcement system) aimed
at better rollover strength and torsional rigidity without sacrificing the
durability off-road recovery work demands.[@wikipedia-mitsubishi-pajero] The
Super Select 4WD system gained fully electronic, on-the-fly switching and a
50:50 front-rear torque split option, badged Super Select 4WD II. A 2003
facelift brought power increases across most of the range and moved the
diesel's intercooler to the front of the engine.

Three trims are recorded here, all from after the 2003 facelift: the
Japanese-market 3.5-litre GDI V6, the North American 3.8-litre V6 that
replaced it in export markets, and the direct-injection 3.2-litre
turbodiesel. Every power figure in the source is published directly in kW.
Dimensions vary by body style, model year and market to an unusual degree
even for this catalogue; the pre-facelift five-door figures are recorded at
generation level, with the facelift and three-door variants noted in each
field.
