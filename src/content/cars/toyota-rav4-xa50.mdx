Unveiled at the March 2018 New York International Auto Show on Toyota's
TNGA-K platform, shared with the XV70 Camry, the fifth RAV4 dropped diesel
entirely and leaned harder into the hybrid drivetrain that has come to
define the nameplate.[@wikipedia-toyota-rav4] In December 2019 it gained a
plug-in hybrid variant, sold as the RAV4 Prime in North America, whose 302
hp combined output briefly made it the second-quickest model in Toyota's
entire lineup after the GR Supra.

Both trims record combined, published system power figures rather than a
sum of engine and motor outputs. That is the same discipline this site applies to
every hybrid. The Prime's torque figure, by contrast, is the combustion
engine alone: Toyota publishes an uprated engine torque figure for the
Prime over the standard hybrid, but no combined system torque figure for
either, so this entry does not invent one.
