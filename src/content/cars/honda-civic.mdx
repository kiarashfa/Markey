Honda's compact car since 1972, and the vehicle that established the
company as a serious carmaker rather than a motorcycle firm branching
out.[@wikipedia-honda-civic] Its CVCC engine met 1970s American emissions
standards without a catalytic converter, at a time when nearly every
domestic rival needed one. This is an early technical reputation the Civic has
carried ever since.
