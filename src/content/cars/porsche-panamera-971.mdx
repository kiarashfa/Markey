Revealed in Berlin in June 2016, the second Panamera grew slightly in every
dimension and moved onto the Volkswagen Group's new MSB
platform.[@wikipedia-porsche-panamera] It dropped the first generation's
liftback-wagon look for a shape with a clearer visual link to the 911, and
added a proper estate body of its own — the Sport Turismo shooting-brake —
alongside the saloon.

Three trims are recorded here: the base turbocharged V6, the twin-turbo V6
Panamera 4S, and the twin-turbo V8 Panamera Turbo. In September 2017 the
Panamera Turbo S E-Hybrid Sport Turismo, combining this generation's V8 with
an electric motor for 680 PS combined, became the most powerful production
estate car sold anywhere — beating the Mercedes-AMG E63 S and Audi RS6
Performance of the day — but its extra electric-motor figures are not
recorded here as a separate trim, since power and torque are the only
consistent axis across every trim in this entry.

In September 2018 Porsche announced it would drop diesel power from the
Panamera range entirely, following the discovery that certain 4S Diesel
models exceeded permitted emissions limits during independent testing.
