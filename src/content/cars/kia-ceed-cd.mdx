The third and, so far, most recent Ceed, revealed ahead of the 2018 Geneva
Motor Show and the first generation to drop the apostrophe from the
"Cee'd" spelling.[@wikipedia-kia-ceed] It spawned two related but distinct
cars on the same platform: the Proceed, a shooting-brake body style built
until the end of 2024, and the XCeed, a crossover that remains in
production after the hatchback and estate ended in January 2026.

Four engines are recorded here, from a 1.0-litre three-cylinder up to a
1.6-litre diesel, all with figures taken from Kia's own published UK
specification pages rather than a prose estimate. Kerb weight is left as an
explicit placeholder: unlike the first and second generations, whose
infoboxes both give a weight range, this generation's does not state one at
all.
