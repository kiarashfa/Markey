Announced in October 1992 on a shortened Legacy floorpan, the first Impreza
was sold as a sedan and wagon from launch, with a coupe added in
1995.[@wikipedia-subaru-impreza] Its lasting reputation, though, comes from
the turbocharged WRX and its homologation-special STI derivatives, campaigned
by Subaru's World Rally Championship team through three consecutive
manufacturers' titles. Japanese-market STI output was nominally capped at
280 PS under an industry gentlemen's agreement the article says most
manufacturers quietly exceeded. The widebody 22B STI, built in just 400
units for Subaru's 40th anniversary, is the version most collectors and
enthusiasts point to first.

Three trims are recorded here: the original November 1992 WRX, the 22B STI,
and the final Version VI STI. Every horsepower figure in the source's WRX
and STI sections is explicitly given in PS with its own kW conversion built
in, so none of the conversions here carry reading ambiguity. The 22B's
torque is published only in kilogram-metres, an older unit this catalogue
converts explicitly rather than silently.
