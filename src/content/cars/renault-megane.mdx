The Mégane has been Renault's C-segment family car since 1995, replacing the
Renault 19 and running to five generations across three decades — sold at
various points as a hatchback, saloon, estate, coupé and convertible, and
carrying the brand's Renault Sport hot-hatch flagship for two of them.[@wikipedia-renault-megane]
The nameplate has also been lent to unrelated models: the original Mégane
Scénic MPV was renamed Scénic within a few years, and a rebadged Renault
Samsung was briefly sold as the Mégane Conquest in the former Yugoslavia. In
2022 the name passed to a battery-electric hatchback, the Mégane E-Tech
Electric, built on Renault's dedicated CMF-EV platform.[@wikipedia-renault-megane-e-tech-electric]
This catalogue covers the first generation, the Renault Sport-era second
generation, and the current E-Tech Electric.
