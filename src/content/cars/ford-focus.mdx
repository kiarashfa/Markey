Ford's compact family car, on sale globally from 1998 as the replacement
for the Escort in Europe and North America and the Laser in Asia and
Oceania.[@wikipedia-ford-focus-first-generation] Its "New Edge" styling
split opinion at launch, but its Control Blade independent rear suspension
became a genuine industry benchmark — rivals, including Volkswagen's Golf
Mk5, followed with multi-link designs of their own within a few years.

This entry covers the first two generations: the original C170 of 1998,
home to the first-ever Focus RS, and the C307 of 2004, whose own RS used a
Volvo-derived turbocharged five-cylinder. Ford ended Focus production in
2025; every generation after the C307 is a gap in this catalogue.
