The first generation, built at Subaru's Gunma plant from January 2012 to March
2021 and sold as the Toyota 86, GT86, FT86, Scion FR-S and Subaru BRZ.[@wikipedia-toyota-86]

A naturally aspirated flat-four making 200 PS in a car weighing 1,190 kg, with
a 53/47 weight distribution and a deliberately low centre of gravity. It is
the reference car for this site's physics: every figure below is published and
cited, which makes it the one entry where the modelled numbers in Test Drive
can be checked against reality rather than merely trusted.[@wikipedia-toyota-86; @wikipedia-drag-coefficients]
