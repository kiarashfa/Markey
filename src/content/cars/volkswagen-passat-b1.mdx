The original Passat, styled by Giorgetto Giugiaro at Italdesign and built on
mechanicals developed with the Audi 80: front-wheel drive with the engine
mounted longitudinally, unusual for a Volkswagen of the period.[@wikipedia-volkswagen-passat-b1]
North America received the same car as the Dasher, with its own engine
lineup and a facelift schedule that briefly ran ahead of the European car's.

Wikipedia's article for this generation runs an unusually complete engine
table, giving power, torque and top speed in km/h directly for nine engine
variants; nothing here needed to be converted from an imperial figure, a
rarer situation than usual for a 1970s car.
