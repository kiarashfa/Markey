The Typ 8L was Audi's first small car in nearly two decades, launched as a
three-door-only hatchback to keep it visually distinct from its Volkswagen
Golf Mk4 platform-mate, with a five-door added in 1999.[@wikipedia-audi-a3] It
shared its dashboard with the first-generation SEAT León.

No period kerb weight is recorded here. Wikipedia's detailed per-engine
specification table for this generation gives power, torque, 0-100 km/h time
and top speed for every variant, but no weight figures, and the Typ 8L was
never sold in Canada or the US, so the NHTSA Canadian Vehicle Specifications
register — otherwise a reliable fallback for this site — has nothing on it
either. The gap is left as a gap rather than filled with a later generation's
figure.
