The original, launched in November 1966 as a rear-wheel-drive 1.1-litre
saloon aimed squarely at the family that had just been able to afford its
first car.[@wikipedia-toyota-corolla-e10] It reached the United States in
March 1968, at around $1,700, and was built in seven countries within two
years of launch. This was an export ambition well beyond what a Japanese compact
of the period usually attempted.

The sourcing here is thinner than later generations: Wikipedia's own article
gives no kerb weight and no torque figure for either engine, only power in
period PS. Both are recorded honestly as gaps rather than filled with a
plausible-looking guess. The 13 PS gained by the twin-carburettor SL model
introduced at the 1968 facelift is the one figure the article states as a
clean delta, so it is recorded as its own trim.
