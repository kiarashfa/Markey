Toyota's mid-size answer to the Honda Accord, launched in 1982 specifically
sized to out-wheelbase it, and for decades the best-selling car in
America.[@wikipedia-toyota-camry] Where the Corolla stayed a compact
throughout its life, the Camry grew from a narrow-bodied Japanese-market
sedan shared with the Toyota Vista into the wide, saloon-only mid-size car
sold worldwide today.
