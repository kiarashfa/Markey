The fourth-generation Tucson, revealed in September 2020 with sharply
faceted bodywork and a "parametric jewel" grille that hides its daytime
running lights inside a geometric pattern of chrome facets.[@wikipedia-hyundai-tucson]
It is built on a shortened version of the Santa Fe's platform and, unusually,
sold in two different lengths at once: a short wheelbase for Europe and the
Middle East, and a long wheelbase for most other markets including North
America, China and Korea.

Only the two straightforward petrol engines are recorded here; Hyundai also
sells hybrid and plug-in hybrid versions of this generation, whose system
outputs are published figures rather than sums of engine and motor, and are
left for a future pass rather than approximated. Dimensions and weight are
each published as ranges spanning wheelbase and trim, so the lowest figure
in each range is recorded at generation level, not assigned to a specific
trim.
