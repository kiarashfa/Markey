The car that was supposed to make Tesla ordinary: a mid-size saloon, built in
volume, sold to people who were not buying a statement.[@wikipedia-tesla-model-3]
It is also the hardest car here to write an honest specification for. Tesla
publishes no power or torque figures at all, so where you see an output on
these pages it comes from the United States EPA's test records rather than
from the manufacturer.
