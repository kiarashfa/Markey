Unveiled in Tokyo on 2 September 2026, five years after the fourth
generation ended production, the new Pajero returns to body-on-frame
construction on a modified front section of the sixth-generation Mitsubishi
Triton's ladder frame, aimed squarely at the Toyota Land
Cruiser.[@wikipedia-mitsubishi-pajero] It launches with a single 2.4-litre
turbodiesel engine and Mitsubishi's Super All Wheel Control system,
carrying over the Super Select 4WD II hardware from the outgoing generation.
Depending on market, it is sold with rear-wheel drive as an entry
specification — a first for the nameplate — or four-wheel drive as
standard. Production is centred on Thailand, for sale in roughly 100
countries; as of this entry, no US or Canadian launch has been announced.

One trim is recorded here, the single powertrain offered at launch. Torque
is published as a range depending on variant (470-480 N⋅m); the lower figure
is recorded, in keeping with this catalogue's rule for a published range not
assigned to a specific trim by the source.
