Styled by Luc Donckerwolke with a sharp-edged "arrow design" that broke from
every Ibiza before it, the fourth generation was the first Volkswagen Group
model on the PQ25 platform later shared with the Volkswagen Polo and Audi
A1.[@wikipedia-seat-ibiza] It launched as a five-door in mid-2008, followed
by the three-door SportCoupé and, from 2010, the ST estate. In June 2009 the
range gained its performance flagships: the FR, tuned to 150 PS, and the
Cupra and limited Bocanegra editions sharing the same 1.4-litre turbocharged
engine at 180 PS.

Three trims are recorded here: the mainstream 1.2 TSI, the 1.4 TSI Cupra hot
hatch, and the 1.6 TDI diesel. Kerb weight and length are both published only
as ranges across engine variants and body styles; the lowest figure in each
case is recorded at generation level rather than assigned to a specific
trim.
