The eighth-generation Hilux, on Toyota's IMV platform, launched simultaneously
in Thailand and Australia in 2015 and has since been revised through three
separate facelifts, each raising the 2.8-litre diesel's output — most
notably the 2020 update that took the automatic-transmission 1GD-FTV from
177 hp to 204 PS.[@wikipedia-toyota-hilux] It has never been sold in North
America, where the Tacoma covers the same role.

As with several long-running trucks and SUVs on this site, only one weight
range is published for the whole engine line-up — 1,495-2,286 kg — rather
than a figure per trim. The lighter rear-wheel-drive petrol and the heavier
four-wheel-drive diesel recorded here take the two ends of that range on the
same basis used elsewhere: a plausible mapping given which trim is
mechanically heavier, not a manufacturer-confirmed figure for those two
engines specifically.
