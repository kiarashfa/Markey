The original Sandero, developed on a shortened version of the Logan's B0
platform and revealed first in Brazil in December 2007, as a Renault — the
first Renault model ever launched outside Europe before its own model
line.[@wikipedia-dacia-sandero] Its European Dacia debut followed at the
2008 Geneva Motor Show.

Five engines are recorded here, including a genuine factory bi-fuel version
of the 1.4-litre petrol engine running on LPG — a detail this catalogue
otherwise has few examples of. Every power figure is published in PS and
converted here at the standard metric rate; kerb weight and dimensions are
each published as ranges spanning the plain hatchback and the raised-
suspension Stepway together, so the lowest figure in each range is recorded
at generation level rather than assigned to a specific trim.
