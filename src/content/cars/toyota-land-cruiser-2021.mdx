The J300, launched in 2021 on Toyota's body-on-frame GA-F platform, is about
200 kg lighter than the J200 it replaced despite Toyota keeping the exterior
footprint close to its predecessor's to preserve the same off-road approach
and departure angles.[@wikipedia-toyota-land-cruiser] It shares its platform
with the Lexus LX, the related Land Cruiser Prado (J250), and — more
distantly — the Toyota Tundra and Sequoia pickups.

As with several long-running SUV nameplates on this site, only one weight
range is published for the whole engine line-up — 2,310-2,645 kg — rather
than a figure per trim. The lighter petrol VJA300 and the heavier diesel
FJA300 take the two ends of that range on the same basis used for the Audi Q7
and A8 entries: a plausible mapping given which trim is mechanically heavier,
not a manufacturer-confirmed figure for those two engines specifically.
