Like the 4, the 5 is really two unrelated cars sharing a name across a long
gap: the original, designed in his spare time by stylist Michel Boué and
sold from 1972 to 1996 across two generations, and the Renault 5 E-Tech, a
battery-electric hatchback that revived the name and much of the original's
styling language in 2024.[@wikipedia-renault-5][@wikipedia-renault-5-e-tech]
The original spawned the mid-engined, rear-wheel-drive Renault 5 Turbo rally
car, which shared little beyond its door panels with the road car it was
named after; the new one has already won European Car of the Year. This
catalogue covers the first-generation original and the current E-Tech.
