Audi's compact executive saloon, launched in 1994 to replace the Audi 80 and
retired in 2025 in favour of a saloon sold under the A5 name
instead.[@wikipedia-audi-a4] Markey covers the nameplate's first and last
generations: the B5 of 1994, which set the template of a chrome single-frame
grille, front-drive base cars and quattro further up the range that every A4
since has followed; and the B9 of 2015, the final car built before Audi
folded even-numbered saloon nameplates into its odd-numbered coupé line. The
three generations between them — B6, B7 and B8 — are not yet catalogued here.
