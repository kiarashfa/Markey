Hyundai's European-market compact hatchback since 2007, developed in
Rüsselsheim and sharing its underpinnings with the Kia Cee'd throughout its
life.[@wikipedia-hyundai-i30] It has been sold in North America under
different names entirely (Elantra Touring, then Elantra GT) while carrying
the i30 badge everywhere else. This catalogue covers the original FD and the
current third-generation PD, launched in 2016.
