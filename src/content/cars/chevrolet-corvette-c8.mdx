The first mid-engined Corvette, and GM's first mid-engined production car of
any kind since the Pontiac Fiero: a decision the company had reportedly
considered and rejected for decades, finally made in 2020 on an all-new
aluminium structure with no manual transmission offered at
all.[@wikipedia-chevrolet-corvette-c8] The naturally aspirated Stingray and
Z06 sit alongside newer hybrid variants — the E-Ray and Grand Sport X add a
front electric motor for all-wheel drive, and the range-topping ZR1X pairs
that with a twin-turbocharged flat-plane V8 — none of which are broken out as
separate trims here because Chevrolet does not publish a combined output
figure for the front motor and rear engine independent of each other.

Every acceleration figure Chevrolet or the press quotes for this car is a
0-60 mph time, never a 0-100 km/h time measured on its own terms, so no
zero-to-100 km/h figure is recorded for any C8 trim here — converting the
imperial figure would misstate a different test as if it were this one.
