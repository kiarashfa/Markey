Launched for the 2018 model year with sharper, more conventional styling
and Nissan's e-Pedal one-pedal driving mode, the second Leaf kept the
first's basic mechanical layout while addressing its two biggest
complaints: modest range and modest power.[@wikipedia-nissan-leaf] A
larger 62 kWh e+ battery and 160 kW motor followed in January 2019,
sold as the Leaf e+ outside North America and the Leaf Plus within it.

Both trims recorded here draw their range and consumption figures from
EPA test records for the specific US-market variant named in Wikipedia's
own citations, which agree with the figures given in the article's
running text.
