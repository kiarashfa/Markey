Hyundai's compact crossover since 2004, launched as a smaller, unibody
alternative to the body-on-frame Santa Fe and named for the Arizona
city.[@wikipedia-hyundai-tucson] It shared its first-generation platform with
the contemporary Kia Sportage, a badge-engineering relationship the two
nameplates have kept up ever since. This catalogue covers the original JM of
2004 and the current fourth-generation NX4, sold since 2020 in short- and
long-wheelbase forms depending on market.
