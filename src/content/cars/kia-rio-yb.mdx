The fourth-generation Rio, revealed at the 2016 Paris Motor Show and, for
the first time, an export-only design — South Korea itself was offered the
related Stonic crossover instead.[@wikipedia-kia-rio] Global production ran
until December 2023; a related but mechanically different car, built on a
longer wheelbase for Russia and China under the FB/UC codes and sold there
as the Kia K2, continued for longer and is not this generation.

The North American engine swap partway through this car's life is recorded
as two separate trims rather than one, because the two are genuinely
different engines with different published outputs (130 hp GDi, then 120 hp
MPi) rather than a running change to the same unit. Dimensions and kerb
weight are each published as ranges spanning body style and trim, so the
lowest figure in each range is recorded at generation level, not assigned to
a specific trim.
