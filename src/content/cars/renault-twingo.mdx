The Twingo has been Renault's smallest car for over three decades, but the
name has covered three genuinely different vehicles rather than one that
evolved. The original, launched in 1993, was a one-box front-engined hatchback
with a single, unmatched trim level and a "smile" of a front end that Renault
never quite repeated. Its 2014 successor kept the shape's cues but moved the
engine to the rear and drove the rear wheels (a layout it shared with its
co-developed cousin, the Smart Fortwo) before the whole line was retired in
2024 as it could not be made to meet upcoming Euro 7 rules affordably.[@wikipedia-renault-twingo]
A fourth-generation Twingo, this time a battery-electric city car whose
styling deliberately echoes the 1993 original, arrived in 2026.[@wikipedia-renault-twingo-e-tech]
This catalogue covers the first, third and current generations; the
Clio-based second generation is the one gap in an otherwise continuous
lineage.
