The GR Supra's engines, transmissions and much of its platform came from
BMW: the four-cylinder B48 and six-cylinder B58 also power various 3 and 4
Series cars, and the Supra was built alongside its closest relative, the BMW
Z4, at Magna Steyr's plant in Graz.[@wikipedia-toyota-gr-supra] Production
ended in March 2026 with a Final Edition, closing this generation's run at
just over seven years.

Both output figures recorded here are launch (2019) specifications, not the
highest ever offered. Toyota progressively raised power through the run:
the six-cylinder alone went through outputs as high as 324 kW for the 2023
Japanese-market RZ and 429 hp for the Final Edition sold outside the US,
and treating every one of those as a separate trim would sprawl well beyond
what this entry needs; the launch 2.0 and 3.0 bracket the range instead.
