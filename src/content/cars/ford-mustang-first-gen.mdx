The original Mustang, built from the parts bin of the humble Ford Falcon but
given its own body, its own name, and a launch at the 1964 World's Fair that
made it the fastest-selling new model in Ford's history.[@wikipedia-ford-mustang-first-generation]

Wikipedia's article treats the whole 1964-1973 run as one generation, even
though the car changed size substantially at facelifts in 1967, 1969 and
1971: the "1971" big-block Mustang shares almost nothing dimensionally with
the original. This entry records only the dimensions and trims of the
original 1964½/1965 launch car rather than blur nine years of growth into
one average figure.
