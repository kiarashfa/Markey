Alec Issigonis's answer to the 1956 Suez fuel crisis: get everything except
the passengers and their luggage out of the way, by turning the engine
sideways and putting the gearbox in the sump underneath it.[@wikipedia-mini]
That single layout decision (a transverse engine driving the front wheels,
with four-fifths of the car's floor left for people) became the template
every supermini since has copied. The car it produced stayed in production,
essentially unaltered underneath, for forty-one years.
