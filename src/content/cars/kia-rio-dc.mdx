The original Rio, launched in November 1999 with engines licensed from
Mazda rather than developed in-house — Kia's own engine programme came
later.[@wikipedia-kia-rio] It was, at launch, the least expensive
mass-produced car sold in the United States, and was well liked for its
price but not for its build quality or driving experience.

The 1.3-litre European engine's power figure is a genuine ambiguous-unit
case: Wikipedia states "75 hp" for it with no indication of whether that is
imperial or metric horsepower, unlike the North American engines in the same
infobox, whose figures the article's own conversion templates confirm are
imperial. The European figure is read the same way, by analogy, and marked
as an estimate rather than a plain verified conversion — the alternative
reading would only move the result by about 2%, but the source itself does
not settle it. Kerb weight is only published as a range across the whole
line-up (1,019-1,256 kg), so the lowest figure is recorded at generation
level rather than assigned to a specific trim.
