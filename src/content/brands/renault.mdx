Founded in Boulogne-Billancourt in 1899 by Louis Renault and his brothers,
and for most of the twentieth century a state-owned company after its 1945
nationalisation (a status carried over from the Renault family's collaboration
with the German occupation, and not fully unwound until a 1996
privatisation).[@wikipedia-renault] Today Renault Group also owns Alpine and
Dacia, and the French state and Nissan each still hold a stake, a legacy of
the alliance the two companies formed in 1999.
