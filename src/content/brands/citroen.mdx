Founded by André Citroën in June 1919, and for its first fifty years the most
technically adventurous mass-market carmaker in Europe.[@wikipedia-citroen]
Front-wheel drive, monocoque construction and self-levelling hydropneumatic
suspension all reached ordinary buyers through Citroën first, usually at some
cost to the company's finances.
