Older than the automobile itself: Peugeot began as a family ironworks in
1810, made bicycles and coffee mills before cars, and only incorporated a
dedicated automobile company, Automobiles Peugeot, in 1896.[@wikipedia-peugeot]
The lion badge (adopted for saw blades in the 1850s, long before the first
Peugeot car) is one of the oldest trademarks still in continuous automotive
use.

Peugeot merged with Citroën in 1976 to form the PSA Group, and today sits
inside Stellantis alongside Citroën, Fiat, Vauxhall/Opel and a dozen other
marques, still headquartered at Sochaux, France, where its cars have been
built since the 1910s.
