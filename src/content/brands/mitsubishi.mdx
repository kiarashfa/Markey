Mitsubishi Motors was spun out of Mitsubishi Heavy Industries' automobile
division in 1970, inheriting a business that had been building cars, trucks
and buses under the wider Mitsubishi conglomerate since the early twentieth
century.[@wikipedia-mitsubishi-motors] It built its reputation on rugged,
rally-proven 4x4s and export-friendly compact cars rather than domestic
luxury saloons, and today sells worldwide with Nissan and Renault as
alliance partners.
