Founded by Soichiro Honda in October 1946 as a technical research institute
repairing motorcycles, and incorporated as a car and motorcycle manufacturer
in 1948.[@wikipedia-honda] Honda has been the world's largest motorcycle
manufacturer since 1959, and its car business (the one this catalogue
covers) grew alongside it into a major global manufacturer known for
small, efficient engines.
