SEAT was founded in 1950 by Spain's state industrial holding company,
Instituto Nacional de Industria, building Fiat designs under licence for a
domestic market that import tariffs had closed off.[@wikipedia-seat] That
arrangement ended in the early 1980s, when Volkswagen took a stake and then
full ownership, folding SEAT into the Volkswagen Group as its value-focused
Spanish marque. SEAT has since spun off its own performance sub-brand, Cupra,
which as of this entry sells alongside SEAT rather than having replaced it.
