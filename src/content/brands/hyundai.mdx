Founded in Seoul in December 1967 by Chung Ju-yung, Hyundai began building
cars under licence from Ford before launching the Pony in 1975 (the first
mass-produced South Korean car sold under a Korean
brand).[@wikipedia-hyundai-motor-company] It closes a real gap in this
catalogue's origin taxonomy: no South Korean marque had previously been
catalogued here.

Hyundai is the flagship brand of the Hyundai Motor Group, which also owns
Kia and the luxury marque Genesis; Hyundai itself is now one of the
world's largest carmakers by volume, with plants across Asia, Europe and
the Americas.
