The modern Bugatti is a revival, not a continuation: Ettore Bugatti's
original Molsheim company built cars from 1909 until it was sold to
Hispano-Suiza in 1963, and the marque then lay dormant for decades before
Volkswagen Group registered Bugatti Automobiles in 1998 and built a new
factory on the same Molsheim site to develop what became the
Veyron.[@wikipedia-bugatti-automobiles] Bugatti is now a joint venture with
Rimac, styled Bugatti Rimac, though cars are still sold under the Bugatti
name alone.
