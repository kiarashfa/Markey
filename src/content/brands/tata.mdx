Founded in 1945 as Tata Engineering and Locomotive Co. (TELCO), building
trucks and locomotives long before it built a passenger car — the maker of
the Nano is the same company that, in the same decade, bought Jaguar and Land
Rover from Ford.[@wikipedia-tata-motors-passenger-vehicles] It traded as Tata
Motors Limited for most of its life; a 2025 corporate restructuring renamed
the passenger-car business Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles Limited and split
the commercial-vehicle side off separately.
