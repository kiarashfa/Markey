Fabbrica Italiana Automobili Torino was founded in Turin in 1899 by Giovanni
Agnelli and became, for most of the twentieth century, the car Italy actually
drove: a mass manufacturer whose small, cheap models were licensed and
copied across the Eastern Bloc and Latin America almost as often as they
were sold under the Fiat badge itself.[@wikipedia-fiat] It now trades as
FIAT Automobiles, a subsidiary of Stellantis.
