DeLorean Motor Company existed for barely seven years, founded in 1975 by
former General Motors executive John DeLorean and gone into receivership by
October 1982, having built one car.[@wikipedia-delorean-motor-company] That
car, the stainless-steel DMC-12, was assembled in a purpose-built factory in
Dunmurry, Northern Ireland, funded substantially by the British government
as a job-creation scheme, a deal that unravelled along with the company
after John DeLorean's arrest on drug-trafficking charges in October 1982,
of which he was later acquitted. The DMC-12 would have been a footnote had
Robert Zemeckis not cast one as a time machine three years later.
