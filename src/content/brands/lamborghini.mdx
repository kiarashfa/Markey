name: Lamborghini
---

Founded in 1963 by tractor manufacturer Ferruccio Lamborghini, who by his
own account started building cars in part to compete directly with
Ferrari.[@wikipedia-lamborghini] The company passed through several owners
after Lamborghini himself sold out in the early 1970s (including a period
in receivership) before Audi bought it in 1998, and it has been part of
the Volkswagen Group, under Audi, ever since.
