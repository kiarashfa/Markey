VEB Sachsenring Automobilwerke Zwickau built cars on the site of the former
Auto Union/DKW works, inheriting DKW's two-stroke engine after Auto Union
re-established itself in the West as what became Audi.[@wikipedia-trabant] It
made essentially one car — the Trabant, in a handful of names — for the whole
of East Germany's existence as a separate state, and stopped within a year of
reunification once government subsidy for its labour-intensive production
line disappeared.
