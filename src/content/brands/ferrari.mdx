Founded by Enzo Ferrari in Modena in September 1939 as Auto Avio
Costruzioni, a name chosen because a non-compete clause from Ferrari's
departure from Alfa Romeo barred him from putting his own name on a car for
four years.[@wikipedia-ferrari] The first car to carry the Ferrari badge
itself, the 125 S, followed in 1947. Road cars have always existed
alongside, and largely to fund, the company's Formula One team, Scuderia
Ferrari, the oldest surviving team in the sport.
