Founded in Mladá Boleslav in 1896 as Laurin & Klement, a bicycle maker that
added motorcycles and then cars, and renamed Škoda after a 1925 merger with
the Škoda Works engineering conglomerate.[@wikipedia-skoda-auto] The
company was nationalised under Czechoslovakia's communist government after
1945, and privatised again after 1989; Volkswagen took a stake in 1991 and
full ownership by 2000, making Škoda the oldest marque in the Volkswagen
Group.
