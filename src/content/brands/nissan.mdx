Traces back to 1933 as Jidosha-Seizo, renamed Nissan Motor the following
year, and to the DAT car company founded two decades earlier still — DAT's
initials survive in the Datsun name Nissan exported under for
decades.[@wikipedia-nissan] Nearly collapsed in the late 1990s, it was
rescued by a capital alliance with Renault in 1999, and the two companies,
later joined by Mitsubishi, have operated as the Renault-Nissan-Mitsubishi
Alliance ever since, each remaining an independently listed company.
