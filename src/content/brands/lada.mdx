AvtoVAZ was founded in Tolyatti, Soviet Union, in 1966, purpose-built with
Fiat's help to mass-produce a Western-designed small car for a domestic
market that had almost nothing like one.[@wikipedia-avtovaz] Cars sold at home
carried the Zhiguli name; the same cars, and Lada as a brand for them, were
built for export from the early 1970s. Lada went on to sell more cars in more
countries than any other Soviet or Russian manufacturer, on the back of low
price and mechanical simplicity rather than sophistication.
