Founded in Rüsselsheim in 1862 as a sewing-machine maker, Opel added
bicycles before it built its first car in 1899, the year recorded here as
its founding, to match the automobile-only scope of this
site.[@wikipedia-opel] It spent
almost ninety years as General Motors' European arm, from 1929 until GM
sold it to the French PSA Group in 2017; PSA's later merger with Fiat
Chrysler folded Opel into Stellantis, its current owner.
