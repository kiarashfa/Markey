Founded in 1911 by racing driver Louis Chevrolet and General Motors founder
William C. Durant, and acquired outright by GM in 1918, Chevrolet has spent
most of its history as GM's high-volume, mainstream marque: the brand built
to sell more cars than Ford, not to sit above
Cadillac.[@wikipedia-chevrolet] Its one enduring exception is the Corvette,
a sports car Chevrolet has built continuously since 1953 and which General
Motors has never let become anything other than a Chevrolet.
