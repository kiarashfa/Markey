Romania's national car brand, founded in 1966 as Uzina de Autoturisme
Pitești to build Renault designs under licence: first the Renault 8 as the
Dacia 1100, then the Renault 12 as the long-running Dacia
1300.[@wikipedia-automobile-dacia] The Romanian state sold the company to
Renault in 1999, and Renault has since repositioned it as its budget brand
worldwide: simple, durable cars sold at a low price rather than a Romanian
nameplate in its own right. Dacia adopted a new khaki-green livery and
wordmark, used throughout this catalogue, in its 2021 rebrand.
