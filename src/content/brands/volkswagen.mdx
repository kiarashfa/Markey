Founded at Wolfsburg in May 1937 to build one car for everybody, and it did:
the Beetle outlasted the state that commissioned it.[@wikipedia-volkswagen]
The company's second act was the harder one: replacing an air-cooled,
rear-engined icon with a water-cooled, front-driven hatchback, and getting it
right first time.
